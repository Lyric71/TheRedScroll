Why we exist

Most agencies sell strategy. Then hand execution to a team that's never opened Douyin.

Great deck. Sharp frameworks. Then a junior team that doesn't really know how RedNote works goes and posts the same content across every platform. Six months later, nobody can explain where the budget went.

We started TheRedScroll because we were tired of watching that happen. We built an agency where the people who make the plan are the same people who post the content. Where the price is fixed and the results are measurable.

Just work that shows up on the platforms. Every day. From China.

Cyril Drouin

Founder and CEO, TheRedScroll

Our mission

We help brands grow on China's social networks.

Not in ways that look good on a slide. In ways they can actually measure. Followers, engagement, conversions.

China's platforms are crowded and expensive. Chinese domestic brands know the market inside out, and that's a tough gap to close from the outside. We close it with a fixed scope, a fixed price, and a team that lives on these platforms.

Our vision

A skilled social media manager with the right AI tools outperforms the old agency model.

The old agency model stacks too many people on top of each other. Planners, copywriters, designers, project managers. Everyone's billing hours. And half of them have never actually posted on Douyin.

We use HubStudio.ai for content production, visual assets, and copy drafts. What used to take a week takes a day. Strategy and cultural context still come from people. That's not going to change, no matter how good the tools get.
