Xiaohongshu (小红书), also called RedNote, accepts applications from overseas
companies directly. The platform says so in its own verification guide.
Eligibility isn't what trips foreign brands up. The paperwork is, along with a
set of naming rules most companies read too late, and a review that quietly
runs three separate checks on a single application.

Here is what to have ready before you start.

| What the platform asks for | Chinese entity | Overseas entity |
|---|---|---|
| Company registration | Business license | BR or CR, both for Hong Kong |
| Translation | Not needed | Chinese translation, company stamp |
| Authorization letter | If the mark sits elsewhere | Same, and one level only |
| Trademark certificate | If the name hits a mark | If the name hits a mark |
| Where you apply | App or desktop | Desktop only |
| Verification fee | 600 yuan a year | 600 yuan a year |

The fee is small. The review is not. Budget four to seven working days for a
clean application and longer than that for a first attempt, because the
platform checks your entity, your account name and your verification details
as three separate things, and any one of them can send the file back.

Every source below was checked twice in September 2026.

## Step 1: pick the account type before you register anything

Xiaohongshu (小红书) splits accounts into professional and non-professional.
Non-professional is the ordinary personal account. Everything commercial sits
on the professional side.

> Xiaohongshu describes the professional account as the entry ticket for
> commercial activity on the platform. A personal professional identity needs
> only real-name verification. An enterprise identity requires a qualification
> review and carries the Blue V badge.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), professional account product guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/fe-platform/68e374e4257bd30bc07eed15c06cb8b9a51f971b.pdf

For a brand, the enterprise identity is the only one that works. It is what
lets you post commercial content without reporting it, run a store and buy
ads. A personal professional account can't do any of that.

There is a second fork inside the enterprise route that almost nobody
explains, and it matters if you want staff posting under your brand.

| Account type | Verified against | Name can be | Per company |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal professional | An ID card | A person | 2 identities |
| Enterprise, company | Company documents | Company, trademark, site or app | 1 identity |
| Enterprise, employee | Company documents | Brand plus a job title | 20 accounts |

> One company entity can authorize multiple employees to verify employee
> accounts. The limit is 20 per entity, raised from the earlier cap of three.
> Employee accounts carry the same rights and functions as the main
> enterprise account.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), enterprise account verification guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/picasso-editor/7072d509ee7370545a056b6f9030a926e8879dca.pdf

One more thing before you touch anything. The account you are logged into when
you apply is the account that gets verified, and you cannot swap it later. The
platform's own guide tells you to register a fresh account for this rather
than converting somebody's personal one. Take that advice. We have watched a
country manager's private account become a corporate asset, and getting it
back is a conversation nobody enjoys.

## Step 2: get your registration and trademark documents right

This is where most of the delay lives. The platform wants originals, in color,
and it wants the details on them to match each other exactly.

For a mainland company, that means the business license with the unified
social credit code. For a foreign company, it means your registration
documents instead.

> Domestic companies upload a business license. Overseas companies upload a
> Business Registration or a Certificate of Incorporation. Hong Kong companies
> need both. Registration documents that are not in Chinese need a Chinese
> translation stamped with the brand owner's company seal.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), Jiguang platform registration and account opening guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/fe-platform/cc8a5f81ddb6d91d6be9dfb34e62b49b999f234b/%E8%81%9A%E5%85%89%E5%B9%B3%E5%8F%B0%E6%B3%A8%E5%86%8C%E4%B8%8E%E5%BC%80%E6%88%B7%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B.pdf

Note the two different stamps. For account verification, a document in a
language other than Chinese or English needs a translation carrying a
translation company's stamp. For ad qualification, the registration document
translation carries the brand owner's own seal. Those are not the same
requirement and they are not interchangeable. Get both versions made at once.

Then the name. Your account name can come from your registered company name, a
trademark, a website or a software application, and it has to pass three
tests: no infringement, not too broad, no ambiguity. "Premium Skincare" fails
the broad test. Your brand name, spelled the way it appears on your trademark
certificate, passes.

If the name you want matches a registered trademark, you upload the
certificate. That certificate has rules of its own.

> A trademark certificate must not be expired. A mark still under application
> needs the acceptance notice, and the application must be at least three
> months old counted from the stamped acceptance date. A mark under transfer
> is accepted only from the company receiving it.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), Jiguang platform registration and account opening guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/fe-platform/cc8a5f81ddb6d91d6be9dfb34e62b49b999f234b/%E8%81%9A%E5%85%89%E5%B9%B3%E5%8F%B0%E6%B3%A8%E5%86%8C%E4%B8%8E%E5%BC%80%E6%88%B7%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B.pdf

That three-month rule catches brands who file a Chinese trademark and try to
open accounts the same week. If you have not filed yet, file now and read [our Xiaohongshu marketing guide for foreign brands](/insights/xiaohongshu-marketing-foreign-brands/) while you wait.

One last trap. If the trademark sits with your head office and the account is
being opened by a different company, you need an authorization letter linking
them. Where the mark is held by an overseas company, the platform accepts a
chain of exactly one level. One. Not two, not a distributor of a distributor.

## Step 3: submit for Blue V verification and pay the fee

Blue V (蓝V) is the badge on a verified Xiaohongshu (小红书) enterprise
account. You get it by completing enterprise verification, and enterprise
verification is a paid service.

> Enterprise account verification costs 600 yuan per application. The
> certification is valid for one year from the date it succeeds, an annual
> review is required to keep it, and the fee is not refunded if the
> application fails.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), enterprise account verification guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026; the annual 600 yuan standard also reported by New Rank (新榜), October 2025. https://a.newrank.cn/trade/news/4199

Read the last line of that again. A failed application still costs you the fee.
It isn't a large sum, but it does mean guessing at the documents is a paid
mistake, and a second attempt is a second 600 yuan.

Foreign companies apply differently from Chinese ones, and this is the detail
that wastes the most time.

> Enterprise verification is open to companies registered in mainland China
> and to overseas, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan companies. The mobile app
> handles mainland entities and first-time verification only. Overseas
> entities, annual reviews and name changes have to be done on the desktop
> site. A single review order is valid for 30 days.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), enterprise account verification guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/picasso-editor/7072d509ee7370545a056b6f9030a926e8879dca.pdf

So if you are outside China, ignore the app entirely for this step. Go to the
business platform in a desktop browser. Teams that start in the app reach the
payment screen, pay, then find the flow will not take their documents.

What happens next is not one review.

> The platform reviews three things separately: the entity's qualifications,
> the account name, and the verification information. A third-party review
> service provider, a named Shanghai information services company, carries out
> the entity checks on the platform's behalf. The applying entity cannot be
> changed once review has started.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), enterprise account verification guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/picasso-editor/7072d509ee7370545a056b6f9030a926e8879dca.pdf

So that is three checks against a single fee, inside one 30-day window.
Payment is by Alipay (支付宝) QR code at the end of the form.

## Step 4: decide whether you need a store, a lead form, or neither

A verified account opens three groups of capability: community features, store
features and paid promotion. Most foreign brands in their first quarter need
the first group and nothing else. Open the store when you have demand, not
before.

If you do want to sell on the platform, there are three store shapes.

Flagship stores are for your own brand or a brand you hold exclusive rights
to. Authorized specialty stores run on an ordinary license from the mark
holder. Collection stores carry several brands at once.

Overseas companies sell through the cross-border route, and the document set
changes again.

> For cross-border trade, every brand needs an authorization letter from the
> brand owner carrying a signature with the signer's job title, or a company
> seal, and the authorization chain must be complete. Where there is no
> authorization, purchase proof for at least one SKU is required at entry.
> A cross-border store entering on purchase proof does not submit a trademark
> certificate. Authorization chains are capped at three levels, two for beauty
> brands.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), merchant qualification requirements, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://picasso-static.xiaohongshu.com/test/8b3bc7324ca06fceea379177f9eed1fa/%E5%B0%8F%E7%BA%A2%E4%B9%A6%E8%B5%84%E8%B4%A8%E5%85%A5%E9%A9%BB%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82.pdf

One detail here will cost you a rename if you miss it. If you plan to bind a
store to the account later, the enterprise account name has to match the store
name. Decide the store name before you submit the account name, not after.

For brands selling a service rather than a product, there is no store. There
is lead collection, which runs through the ad platform and pushes inquiries
into direct messages or a form. That is Step 5.

## Step 5: link the ad account

Paid promotion on Xiaohongshu (小红书) runs through Jiguang (聚光). You cannot
open it first. The professional account has to exist and be verified before
the ad platform will talk to you.

> The sequence is: register the app account and bind a phone number, log into
> the ad platform with that number, choose an advertiser account or an agency
> account, complete professional account verification, then submit promotion
> qualifications in the ad platform. Creative is reviewed after the
> qualifications pass.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), Jiguang platform registration and account opening guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/fe-platform/cc8a5f81ddb6d91d6be9dfb34e62b49b999f234b/%E8%81%9A%E5%85%89%E5%B9%B3%E5%8F%B0%E6%B3%A8%E5%86%8C%E4%B8%8E%E5%BC%80%E6%88%B7%E6%B5%81%E7%A8%8B.pdf

Two small requirements at this stage catch people out. Your industry category
has to match the business scope written on your registration documents, not
the category you think describes you best. And your avatar has to be the brand
logo, with no person in it.

> The ad platform supports four marketing scenarios: product seeding, product
> sales, lead collection and livestream promotion. The account balance and the
> account daily budget are shared with the professional account promotion
> center.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), Jiguang platform product manual, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/fe-platform/37f82988d0e9273cd645bb819c37a4e0f697bc70.pdf

Then the rule that changed everything for foreign advertisers in 2026.

> Since February 2, 2026, overseas merchants moved from self-service account
> opening on the Jiguang ad platform to invitation-only admission. They must
> go through the invitation channel via a Xiaohongshu-authorized agency.
> Source: Ziyouxing Studio (自由行Studio), Xiaohongshu Jiguang overseas guide, updated August 2026. https://zyxstudio.net/articles/xiaohongshu-jiguang-guide.html

If you are an overseas entity, you no longer open an ad account by yourself.
An authorized agency submits for you. [Our RedNote agency page](/rednote-agency/) explains how
that works on our side. Plan for it, because a brand that budgets for paid
support and then discovers it needs an intermediary loses a month.

## The five reasons applications get rejected

We are describing grounds the platform publishes, not a private list. Every
row below traces to platform documentation.

| Reason | What the platform says | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Barred industry | Listed categories cannot verify at all | Check the list before paying |
| Documents invalid or untrue | Treated as a failed review, fee kept | Color originals, matching details |
| Name breaks the rules | Infringing, too broad or ambiguous | Use the trademark spelling |
| Order expired | The 30-day window ran out | Submit with documents ready |
| Entity swapped mid-review | Not permitted once started | Confirm the applicant first |

The first one is the expensive surprise, because the list is longer than
foreign brands expect.

> Categories that cannot obtain enterprise verification include military and
> political items, weapons and controlled goods, most medical and health
> categories, sexual health, tobacco including electronic cigarettes, fortune
> telling, gambling, counterfeit brands, buying agents, campus lending,
> franchise recruitment, overseas maternity agencies and private clubs.
> Medical and health is supported only for health supplements and two device
> types, contact lenses and beauty devices, each with its own extra
> certificate. Finance is limited to members of the central bank and the three
> regulatory commissions.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), enterprise account verification guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/picasso-editor/7072d509ee7370545a056b6f9030a926e8879dca.pdf

Supplements and skincare devices are the two categories where foreign brands
most often assume they are fine and are not. Both are allowed, and both need a
certificate most brands have never heard of.

There is also a penalty that applies to your whole company rather than one
account.

> If all of the enterprise accounts verified under one entity have published
> content seriously violating community norms, that entity is barred from
> verifying enterprise accounts.
> Source: Xiaohongshu (小红书), enterprise account verification guide, platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://fe-video-qc.xhscdn.com/picasso-editor/7072d509ee7370545a056b6f9030a926e8879dca.pdf

## What changes if you have no Chinese entity

Less than you would expect, and more than the platform admits in one place.

You can verify. The enterprise guide names overseas and Hong Kong, Macau and
Taiwan companies as eligible, and the ad platform's document list has a column
for you. What changes is the route.

You apply on the desktop site, never the app. You file a Business Registration
or a Certificate of Incorporation instead of a business license, and both if
you are a Hong Kong company. You add translations with the right stamp on
each. You sell through the cross-border store route rather than a domestic
one. And since February 2026 you reach the ad platform through an authorized
agency rather than opening the account yourself.

The place where the absence of a Chinese company genuinely bites is the
trademark chain. Where a mark is held by your overseas parent and the account
is opened by another company, the platform accepts one level of
general-distributor authorization and no more. If your structure runs head
office to regional office to local distributor, the local distributor cannot
be the account holder. Fix the structure or file the mark locally. [Our China market entry service](/services/market-entry/) exists largely because of problems shaped like this one.

The platform publishes its own review times, which is rarer than it should be.

> After an enterprise professional account application is submitted, a review
> specialist makes contact within one working day, and review is completed in
> four to seven working days on average. A single review order is valid for
> 30 days.

Add your own preparation time in front of that. Getting certified translations

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I open a Xiaohongshu business account without a Chinese company?

Yes. Xiaohongshu (小红书) names overseas companies and Hong Kong, Macau and
Taiwan companies as eligible in its enterprise verification guide. You apply on
the desktop business site rather than the app, and you file a Business
Registration or Certificate of Incorporation instead of a mainland business
license. Hong Kong companies file both. Any document not in Chinese needs a
stamped translation.

### What does Blue V verification cost and how long does it take?

Verification costs 600 yuan per application. It is valid for one year from
approval, and an annual review keeps it live. The fee is not refunded if the
application fails. The platform says a reviewer makes contact within one
working day and that review takes four to seven working days on average. Your
review order expires after 30 days.

### Why was my Xiaohongshu application rejected?

Five published grounds cover most cases: your category is on the barred
industry list, your documents were invalid or did not match, your account name
broke the naming rules, your 30-day review order expired or the applying entity
was changed after review started. The fee is not returned, so check the
industry list and your document details before paying.
