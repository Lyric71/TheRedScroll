first call. With named clients, you can ask someone else how it went. The
platform's agency list is the record Xiaohongshu (小红书) keeps of the firms it
works with on creator orders. Location matters for a plainer reason: a team in
mainland China writes notes the way Chinese users write them.

### What "official" means on Xiaohongshu

Paid creator work runs through Pugongying (蒲公英), the platform's creator
marketplace. Its help center publishes a list of the agencies cleared to work on
it.

> Xiaohongshu's 2026 Pugongying agency list names 149 companies, each with an
> introduction and contact details.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center (小红书蒲公英帮助中心), October 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

The list covers creator orders only. Ad accounts on Juguang (聚光), the ad
platform, are a separate matter. It's in Chinese, under Chinese company names.
An English brand name will not appear on it. Ask any agency for its Chinese
legal name, then search the list yourself.

The platform also lets you control who acts in your name.

> An agency can only order creators in a brand's name if the brand authorizes it
> in Pugongying. The brand can stop the authorization at any time, and it takes
> effect at once.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, August 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

Orders already placed stay in force when you end an authorization. Only new
orders stop. To give that authorization, you log in to Pugongying with your own
professional account (专业号).

> A brand logs in to Pugongying with its Xiaohongshu professional account. The
> account review costs 600 yuan, is not refunded if it fails, and must be passed
> again every year.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, October 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

That leaves the word "official." Pugongying's rules for clients, agencies
included, forbid suggesting a closer tie to the platform than the one that
exists.

> Pugongying clients, including agencies, may not imply any authorization,
> agency, endorsement or other special relationship with the platform beyond
> their actual cooperation.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, September 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

An agency that is not on the list can still do good work. It may write the
content and let you place the orders from your own account. It may also
subcontract a listed agency. Either is fine, as long as it tells you which.

With those checks in hand, the next four sections match each common job to the
agency type that fits it.

## Best for a beauty or fashion launch

Pick a beauty and fashion studio, or a listed agency with a beauty team. In
these launches the creator brief does most of the work. You want briefs that
sound like a user, not a label, and a person who checks every product claim
before a creator posts it.

For skin care, that check is the law.

> Cosmetics efficacy claims need a sufficient scientific basis, and a summary of
> the evidence must be published on a regulator-designated website.
> Source: State Council (国务院), Cosmetics Supervision and Administration Regulation, Order 727, Article 22, in force January 2021. https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2020-06/29/content_5522593.htm

Side-by-side product tests carry their own risk on Xiaohongshu (小红书) this year.

> On July 30, 2026, Xiaohongshu's commercial governance team launched a
> crackdown on side-by-side tests that quietly run down rival products. It gave
> brands and creators seven days to fix or delete such posts.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center (小红书蒲公英帮助中心), July 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

Get a recent launch brief from each candidate and read how it treats rival
products. A brief that asks creators to test your serum beside a rival's, and
then pick holes in the rival, is now a liability.

Then ask how creator fees are billed. A custom order on Pugongying (蒲公英) carries
the creator's own price plus a platform fee.

> A Pugongying custom order costs the creator's fixed price plus a 10% platform
> service fee: 10,000 yuan becomes 11,000.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, September 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

That 10% goes to the platform, not the agency, so it gets its own line in the
proposal.

## Best for seeding with many small creators

A small-creator volume shop fits here, but only one that works inside the
platform. Some brands need dozens of everyday users, known as KOCs (key opinion
consumers), each posting a real review. That fills the search results a buyer
sees before a purchase. Pugongying (蒲公英) has modes built for it.

| Pugongying mode              | How creators are picked                  | Who sets the price                     | Fits                          |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Custom order (定制商单)      | The brand picks each creator             | The creator, plus the 10% fee          | Launch content                |
| Batch order (批量商单)       | Creators apply, the brand picks 2 to 100 | The creator, with a 30-day offer price | Volume on a mid budget        |
| Trial recruitment (众测招募) | Creators under 150,000 followers apply   | The brand, fixed or by reads           | Consumer goods, small budgets |

> In batch orders, creators with 1,000 followers or more apply to a brief, and
> the brand picks 2 to 100 of them. Creators may offer 50% to 100% of their
> listed price for 30 days.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, October 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

Volume is also where cheating hides. Xiaohongshu (小红书) cleans it out in bulk.

> Between August 22 and September 20, 2026, Xiaohongshu acted against 76,000
> accounts and 357,000 notes for interaction cheating. It removed 170 million
> fake follows and 980 million fake likes and saves.
> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, September 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

Deals done off the platform now cost the brand, too.

> From January 12, 2026, Xiaohongshu's brand trust system targets creator deals
> made outside the official platform. Measures include a risk warning shown to

> Source: Xiaohongshu Pugongying help center, January 2026. https://pgy.xiaohongshu.com/help/home

So ask where the orders will sit. The right answer is your own Pugongying
account, where you can see every creator, price and note. Log in and look,
rather than accept a screenshot. A spreadsheet of links isn't the same thing.

This is a job for a B2B content team, one that writes search notes and sets up
staff accounts. Business sellers use Xiaohongshu (小红书) too, and many post
through employee accounts, called KOS (key opinion sales).

> More than 40,000 company accounts run employee accounts on Xiaohongshu, and
> more than 6 million leads a month come through them (August 2026).
> Source: Xiaohongshu 2026 KOS guide, via Huaxia Morning News (华夏早报) on ifeng (凤凰网), September 2026. https://baby.ifeng.com/c/8wUkh912dXd

The platform doesn't split those leads into business and consumer. Treat the
number as a sign of scale, not a B2B benchmark.

The risk for B2B is the hand-off. A sales team wants a phone number, and the
platform punishes notes that steer users off the app.

> Xiaohongshu revised its trade-diversion rules effective September 18, 2026.
> Penalties include suspending business cooperation and Pugongying order rights.
> Source: Xiaohongshu e-commerce rules center (小红书电商学习中心), September 2026. https://school.xiaohongshu.com/rule/detail/17/119931

A B2B agency should show you how an inquiry reaches your sales team inside the
platform's rules. Ask to see three real inquiries from past work, with names
removed. View counts don't answer that question.

## Six questions to ask any Xiaohongshu agency before you sign

1. What is your company's Chinese legal name, and is it on the 2026
   Pugongying agency list?
2. Will creator orders sit in our own Pugongying account, under an
   authorization we can end?
3. Which named clients can we call, and what did you do for them on
   Xiaohongshu?
4. Who writes our notes, and in which city do they work?
5. What are the agency fee, the creator fees, the 10% platform fee and the
   ad spend, each on its own line?
6. Who checks product claims and comparison content before a creator posts?

Expect all six answers in writing. If yours is already in place, start with
question two. Moving orders into your own account shows you everything you've
paid for.

## What a good Xiaohongshu proposal looks like

A good proposal reads like a work order. It names the notes, creators and ads
you will get each month, and who approves each one.

Money comes in four kinds, and each needs its own line: the agency's fee,
creator fees, the platform's 10% on creator orders, and ad spend. When two share
a line, ask why.

Accounts come next. Your professional account, your Pugongying orders and your
ad account should all sit in your company's name. Your own staff should hold the
logins. Then nothing has to be handed back when the contract ends (and nothing
can be held back, either). Ask the agency to list each account in the proposal,
with its owner and every person who has access.

Define the numbers. "Engagement" should mean likes, saves and comments, counted
the same way every month. A lead is a person who asked about your product.

Last, the exit. What do you keep, in what format, and by which date?

## Questions buyers ask

### What should a Xiaohongshu agency charge?

No platform, regulator or trade body publishes a market rate for Xiaohongshu
agency fees. Quotes move with scope: notes per month, the number of creators,
claim review and reporting. Keep creator fees, the 10% Pugongying platform fee
and ad spend on separate lines. TheRedScroll publishes its full rate card on its
[pricing page](/pricing/).

### How do I verify a Xiaohongshu agency's case studies?

Ask for the client's name, the dates and the agency's exact role. Then ask to
speak to that client. On Xiaohongshu, search the brand's account and read the
notes from that period. If the agency ran creator orders, ask to see them in the
Pugongying order records, with prices hidden if needed.

### Do I need an agency with a mainland team?

For the writing, yes. Notes have to read as if a Chinese user wrote them. An
overseas-based agency can fit if it names its China writers. For the paperwork,
it depends on where your company is registered. Xiaohongshu verifies businesses
from the mainland, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia and other opened regions,
generally within three working days. Ad accounts for overseas companies are by
invitation.
