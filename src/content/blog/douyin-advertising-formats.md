Your Douyin (抖音) ads spend every day. The cost per result jumps around, and
nobody on the account can say which format is doing the work.

That's a format question first and a bidding question second. Here's what each
product does.

| Format | What it is for | How it is bought | What it costs | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In-feed | Reach, leads, traffic | Auction: CPM, CPC or oCPM | Ours: CPM 10 to 50 yuan, CPC from 0.2 yuan | The workhorse. Buy it first |
| Search | People typing your category | Ocean Engine keyword auction | From 100 yuan a day | Underbought. Pair it with in-feed |
| Live-room ads | Viewers into a live room | Qianchuan auction, ROI target | Prepaid, no published minimum | Only with a staffed room and a shop |
| DOU+ | Boost one video | Self-serve order | 100 yuan to 5 million yuan an order | For testing, not for scaling |
| Brand Takeover | The app-open moment | Reserved, by day or CPM | No current public price | Launch days only |
| TopView | App-open video that drops into the feed | Reserved, by day or CPM | 52.8 million yuan a day list price (2020) | A dated launch, with search already live |

Figures marked "ours" are the planning ranges on [our advertising services page](/services/advertising/),
not a platform average, and they cover media spend only. Every other figure is
sourced and dated below, and every source was checked twice in October 2026.

## How Douyin advertising is sold, and who sells it

Douyin (抖音) is TikTok's sister app inside China. Its ads are sold by Ocean
Engine (巨量引擎), ByteDance's ad business, and the bidding platform changed its
name in December 2025.

> From December 22, 2025, Ocean Engine's bidding platform 巨量广告 was renamed 巨量营销
> (Ocean Engine Marketing), and the "ad" level inside a campaign was renamed
> "unit".
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, December 2025. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1852282960952776

Ocean Engine Marketing sells in-feed, search and lead ads, and you pay up front.

> Ocean Engine bills in advance, by CPC, OCPC, CPM, OCPM and other modes. Its
> agreement says the platform sets and publishes the minimum top-up.
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), platform service agreement, in force January 2026. https://sf1-cdn-tos.douyinstatic.com/obj/ies-hotsoon-draft/account_center/ee525166-d060-401d-9d2f-83359617acf0.html

The second auction is Qianchuan (巨量千川), built for selling inside Douyin:
products, shop listings and live rooms. It now belongs to Douyin's e-commerce
business.

> In March 2025 the Qianchuan team formally moved into Douyin E-commerce.
> Source: Xinhua Finance (新华财经), via Sina Finance, December 2025. https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2025-12-15/doc-inhavwnp4307756.shtml

> Qianchuan's agreement lists the in-app boost tool for shops (小店随心推) as one of
> its entry points. It bills by CPC, OCPC, CPM or OCPM, and sets and publishes
> its own minimum top-up.
> Source: Qianchuan (巨量千川), platform service agreement, updated December 2025. https://lf3-cdn-tos.draftstatic.com/obj/ies-hotsoon-draft/qianchuan/a96a6a25-17d8-4083-9529-0c79102aa0b9.html

The reservation book is brand advertising: the app-open formats, sold by the day
or by the thousand views. Neither auction publishes a minimum deposit, so any
floor you're quoted comes from whoever opened the account.

## In-feed ads and the three-second rule

An in-feed ad is a video in Douyin's (抖音) recommended feed, the For You scroll.
The best ones run from the brand's own account and look like a post.

> Ocean Engine's native ads run from the advertiser's own Douyin account and
> keep normal interactions such as follows and profile visits. Ocean Engine says
> this "can maximize ad conversion".
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), native ads manual, December 2022. https://support.oceanengine.com/support/content/130523

Which brings us to the three-second rule. It turns up in most Douyin briefs.
Ocean Engine (巨量引擎) hasn't published a figure for it on any page we could find.

What it does publish points the same way. Its premium format is built around
three seconds, and its spec sheet polices the very first frame.

> Ocean Engine's spec sheet splits TopView into a "first 3s" style and an "after
> 3s" style. For in-feed video, black may cover no more than 60% of the first
> frame.
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), creative spec sheet in the national rate card, October 2020. https://lf3-ttcdn-tos.pstatp.com/obj/cognition-public/attachment_pdf_31204ca42bb9640459f1ace869f45701.pdf

And its 2026 review rules list the openings that get an ad treated as low
quality.

> Ocean Engine's user experience rules list low-quality creative patterns,
> including poor production, an unclear picture, slideshow or PPT-style video
> and large blocks of on-screen text.
> Source: Ocean Engine rules center (巨量广告规则中心), user experience risk rules, January 2026. https://support.oceanengine.com/support/content/137617?spaceId=171

That list reads like a description of a translated Western ad. It opens on a
logo card, then pans slowly over a paragraph of subtitles.

Here is how we build the opening instead. It is our working checklist, not
platform data.

| Seconds | What is on screen | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 1 | A face, a hand or the product in use, mid-action | Stops the thumb. No logo card |
| 1 to 3 | One spoken line in Mandarin, with captions | Names the problem the product fixes |
| 3 to 10 | The proof: a demo, a before and after, a price | Earns the click |
| 10 to the end | The offer and one action | Search the brand, visit the shop, fill the form |

Shoot it vertical, in China, with Chinese speakers. Don't re-cut a global spot
and add subtitles.

## TopView and Brand Takeover: when the premium is worth it

Both formats buy the first thing people see when they open Douyin (抖音). Brand
Takeover is the splash ad (开屏), and it is short.

> A splash ad shows for a fixed time: three seconds for a static image, four for
> an animated one and five for video. It is billed by CPM.
> Source: Ocean Engine help center (巨量引擎帮助中心), May 2020. https://www.oceanengine.com/help/guanggao-zhanxian

TopView is the longer version. It opens the app full screen, then runs on in the
recommended feed, where people can like and share it.

The last public price list is old, so treat it as a sense of scale.

> In its rate card for the fourth quarter of 2020, Ocean Engine listed TopView
> first-refresh at 52.8 million yuan a day. TopView CPM cost 320 yuan in core
> cities, with orders from 200,000 yuan net. TopView took 10 to 60 seconds of
> vertical video.
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), national rate card, October 2020. https://lf3-ttcdn-tos.pstatp.com/obj/cognition-public/attachment_pdf_31204ca42bb9640459f1ace869f45701.pdf

No newer card is public. Our own planning line for TopView starts at the same
200,000 yuan.

One rule covers every app-open ad in China.

> Splash-screen ads shown when an app starts must carry a clear close mark and
> close with one click.
> Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局), Internet Advertising Measures, Order 72, in force May 2023. https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10506/202306/content_6885261.html

So when is the premium worth it? Only for a dated launch, with search ads
already live and a video that works as a normal in-feed post. A TopView sends
people to search your name, and without search ads you don't control what they
find.

## Search ads on Douyin, which most brands ignore

A lot of Douyin (抖音) search starts with a video.

> 53% of Douyin searches come after watching a video. The other 47% are started
> by the user.
> Source: China Daily (中国日报网), reporting Ocean Engine's 2025 search white paper, November 2025. https://caijing.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202511/25/WS69252082a310942cc499329e.html

> Ocean Engine says search traffic converts 90% better than recommended-feed
> traffic, and 72% of users look only at the first screen of results.
> Source: Beijing Daily (京报网), reporting Ocean Engine's 2025 search white paper, November 2025. https://news.bjd.com.cn/2025/11/25/11425198.shtml

Why do brands skip it? They think of Douyin as a feed. Their buyers watch a
video, then type the product name to compare. And the entry cost is low.

> Search ads in the 7-day steady mode need a budget of at least 2,000 yuan.
> Custom schedules need at least 100 yuan a day.
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, September 2024. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1810687227706499

Bid on your brand name first (in Chinese, and in the spelling people actually
type). Then add three to five category terms. Only the first screen counts, so
bid to be on it.

## Live-stream ads and traffic to a room

Live-room ads are bought in Qianchuan (巨量千川), and in 2024 it changed how they're
sold.

> On May 14, 2024, Qianchuan launched omni-channel promotion for products and
> upgraded the live-room version.
> Source: 36Kr (36氪), May 2024. https://m.36kr.com/newsflashes/2775491039724416

Omni-channel promotion (全域推广) is bought against sales, and it takes over the
room.

> For a live room, omni-channel promotion optimizes overall payment ROI: the
> room's total sales during the campaign, divided by its spend. While it runs,
> every other plan promoting that live room is paused.
> Source: Qianchuan (巨量千川), omni-channel promotion agreement, June 2024. https://lf26-cdn-tos.draftstatic.com/obj/ies-hotsoon-draft/qianchuan/dfa2a6ab-7eab-4074-a04f-ce528885576d.html

That second line trips teams up. Switch it on and your older room campaigns
stop.

A live-room campaign needs a room worth sending people to: a host, a shop, stock
and someone answering questions. Without them, the ROI target has nothing to
work with. [Our Douyin marketing cost guide](/insights/douyin-marketing-cost/) prices the staff.

That covers the products. Most of the money is won or lost in the next part.

## How Douyin ad bidding works, plainly

In short, you bid on a result and pay for views.

> Ocean Engine labels oCPM as "目标转化出价-按展示付费": a bid on the target conversion,
> paid per impression.
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, September 2025. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1843402376347850

So you tell Ocean Engine (巨量引擎) what a lead or an order is worth to you. It then
bids on every view for you. It bids higher for people it expects to convert and
lower for everyone else. It charges you per thousand views at the prices those
auctions cleared. When its predictions hold, your average cost per result lands
near the target. When they don't, it drifts, sometimes a long way.

The bid works as a target.

Then you pick a strategy. Ocean Engine set out the options in 2023.

> On the upgraded platform, bid and billing are set at the project level. New
> bid strategies added "max delivery" (放量投放) and "cost cap" (控制成本上限). Billing
> options are CPM, CPC, oCPM and oCPC.
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, February 2023. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1756250989174795

> The stable cost strategy (稳定成本) no longer lets advertisers choose a delivery
> speed and runs at accelerated delivery. Advertisers who want even pacing are
> pointed to the optimal cost strategy (最优成本).
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, June 2023. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1769750815636488

A new ad goes through a learning period while the system works out who converts.
Ocean Engine tracks it as a formal status.

> Ocean Engine's API returns a learning-period status (learning_phase) for each
> ad.
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, December 2022. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1751541589934215

How many conversions end it, and which edits restart it, is not on any open
platform page. The numbers that circulate come from courses and forums, so we
don't repeat them.

What's published is the guarantee. If the system overshoots your target cost by
enough, the platform compensates you in ad credit, under conditions. Qianchuan
(巨量千川) publishes its rules in full.

> Qianchuan's cost guarantee runs from first delivery to midnight on the third
> day. A plan needs at least 6 conversions, or 2 in search, and a cost more than
> 20% over the bid. Bid or targeting may each be changed at most twice a day.
> The refund is paid as ad credit, valid for one year.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), Qianchuan cost guarantee rules, December 2025. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHMNDfneBP7r

> For ROI bidding, the guarantee pays when actual ROI falls below 80% of the
> target. The ROI target or targeting may each be changed at most twice a day.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), Qianchuan ROI cost guarantee rules, December 2025. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aHQbPJNKixFy

The main Ocean Engine platform runs its own version. Its thresholds sit in a
help center that doesn't publish them openly, but the API shows the payout.

> Since June 2024 Ocean Engine's API returns each project's cost guarantee
> status and the amount paid out.
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog, June 2024. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1802994108997770

Then there's one-click boost (一键起量), which buys volume fast.

> Ocean Engine describes one-click boost as "a cost-controllable aggressive
> bid-raising tool".
> Source: Ocean Engine open platform (巨量引擎开放平台), changelog archive, May 2020. https://open.oceanengine.com/changelog/1740034645231629

On Qianchuan, spend and conversions from boost tools don't count toward the
guarantee.

Here's how the settings compare.

| Setting | What you set | What you pay | What protects you |
|---|---|---|---|
| oCPM, stable cost | A target cost per result | Per thousand views | A cost guarantee, if you leave it alone |
| oCPM, cost cap | A ceiling per result | Per thousand views | The ceiling itself |
| Max delivery | A budget | Per thousand views | No cost target to guarantee |
| ROI target (Qianchuan) | Sales per yuan spent | Per thousand views | Credit if ROI lands below 80% of target |
| One-click boost | Extra spend for volume | On top of the plan | Outside the Qianchuan guarantee |

## Creative rules that decide delivery

Two kinds of rule decide whether a Douyin (抖音) ad runs: the law, and the
platform's quality review.

The law first. For a foreign brand, two rules matter most.

> Advertising may not use terms such as "national-level", "highest grade" or
> "best". The fine for an advertiser runs from 200,000 to 1 million yuan.
> Source: Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China (广告法), as amended October 2018, via gov.cn. https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647620.htm

> Paid content that sells through reviews or experience sharing with a purchase
> link must be clearly labeled "广告" (advertisement).
> Source: State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局), Internet Advertising Measures, Order 72, in force May 2023. https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10506/202306/content_6885261.html

"Best", "number one", "world-leading". Global copy is full of them, and each one
is a rejection waiting to happen.

Then the quality review, which is where we see foreign ads stall. An ad can be
legal and still get throttled.

> Qianchuan sorts low-quality content into four classes: exaggeration,
> discomfort, low-quality content and low-quality creative. Measures include
> showing it less, limiting delivery and taking plans offline, and advertisers
> may see spend slow down.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), Qianchuan low-quality content notice, August 2022. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/articlev0/aHVMP6peVA36

The notice dates from 2022 and is still live. The 2026 Ocean Engine (巨量引擎) rules
above cover the same ground.

Overseas advertisers get their own rules, and they keep changing.

> Ocean Engine's rules center posted review-rule changes for overseas
> advertisers on February 10 and July 13, 2026.
> Source: Ocean Engine rules center (巨量广告规则中心), February and July 2026. https://support.oceanengine.com/support/content/206252?spaceId=171 https://support.oceanengine.com/support/content/211775?spaceId=171

Check the current text before each campaign.

In practice:

1. No absolute claims in the copy, the voiceover or the on-screen text.
2. Shoot real footage. Slideshows, subtitle walls and blurry clips are on
   the low-quality list.
3. Label creator content with a purchase link as an ad.
4. Get a Chinese speaker to read every line of on-screen text before review.

## The three mistakes that burn a Douyin budget

When we audit Douyin (抖音) accounts, the same three keep turning up.

**1. Touching the bid every day.** The Qianchuan guarantee allows two bid edits
a day at most and runs to midnight on day three. Set a target, leave it alone
for those three days, then judge.

**2. Paying to push a video that doesn't work on its own.** An ad carries a good
video further. It won't fix a weak one. A slideshow or a subtitle wall hits the
low-quality rules and gets throttled, whatever the bid. Test with DOU+ first and
put money only behind the videos people already watch.

**3. Feed on, search off.** Just over half of Douyin searches start after
someone watches a video. If you aren't on the first screen when they type your
name, you've paid to create interest and someone else can take the click.

Sometimes nobody did anything wrong. A small budget can sit in learning for a
long time, and a category with strict review moves slowly. Check both before you
blame the agency.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does Douyin ad bidding work?

Douyin's auctions bill by CPM, CPC, oCPM or oCPC. With oCPM, you set a target
cost for a result, such as a lead or an order, and pay per thousand views. The
system bids on each view for you. Qianchuan compensates big overshoots in ad
credit, but only if you change the bid or targeting twice a day at most.

### What is a typical CPM on Douyin?

No platform average is published. Our planning range for in-feed is 10 to 50
yuan per thousand views, with CPC from 0.2 yuan. The last public rate card, from
2020, listed TopView at 320 yuan CPM in core cities. Your own account history is
a better benchmark than any market figure.

### Do Douyin search ads work for foreign brands?

Yes, if people search your brand or your category. Ocean Engine says 53% of
Douyin searches come after watching a video, and search traffic converts 90%
better than feed traffic. Entry is low: 100 yuan a day on a custom schedule. Bid
on your brand name first, then a few category terms in natural Chinese.
