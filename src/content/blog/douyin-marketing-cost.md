## Why Douyin marketing cost runs higher than other platforms

Douyin (抖音), TikTok's sister app inside China, is enormous, and reach at that
scale costs money.

> Douyin had about 1.03 billion monthly active users in June 2026, second only
> to WeChat.
> Source: Sina Finance (新浪财经), reporting QuestMobile, August 2026. https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/discovery/2026-08-04/doc-inimcxxp2069391.shtml

> Ocean Engine, ByteDance's ad platform, still tells advertisers that Douyin has
> more than 600 million daily active users.
> Source: China Daily (中国日报网), January 2026. https://caijing.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202601/30/WS697c3a1aa310942cc499d74d.html

Three things push the bill up. Video is the first. Xiaohongshu (小红书) can run on
photo notes and WeChat (微信) on articles. Douyin runs on short video. Every one
of those videos has to be planned, shot, cut and subtitled in Chinese before it
earns a single view.

Then there's paid traffic. Sellers fight for the same feed.

> For every 100 videos a Douyin user watches, about 9 to 12 are e-commerce
> content from the trade and paid traffic pools.
> Source: LatePost (晚点LatePost), via Sina Finance, September 2025. https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/t/2025-09-08/doc-infpuwrf4417383.shtml

And once you sell, there's the live room, which needs hosts and operators, plus
paid traffic every day it runs. Even the specialists earn thin margins.

> Yowant, a listed live commerce company, lifted its social e-commerce gross
> margin from 2.08% to 5.53% in 2025.
> Source: Yowant (遥望科技), 2025 annual report, Shenzhen Stock Exchange, April 2026. https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-28/c6762ed2-b22f-4e6a-93c3-681d924ba0ed.PDF

## Account setup and verification

The verified Douyin (抖音) enterprise account, known as Blue V (蓝V), is the
cheapest line on the list.

> Douyin Blue V verification costs 600 yuan for the first year and 120 yuan a
> year to renew. Review takes about two working days.
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), Douyin enterprise account FAQ, May 2022. https://www.oceanengine.com/faq/douyin-qyh-zmrzn.html

There are two catches. You pay even if verification fails. And the current
agreement only promises that review starts within 15 working days.

> The review fee is whatever the application page shows. It is owed whether or
> not verification succeeds, and review begins within 15 working days.
> Source: Douyin enterprise account verification agreement (抖音企业号认证服务协议), December 2025. https://renzheng.douyin.com/support/content/123631?graphId=412&mappingType=2&spaceId=77

For a foreign brand, the bigger question is the entity. Douyin's 2022 FAQ pages
describe the enterprise account as one for companies in China. In 2026 its help
center added a separate route.

> Douyin's enterprise help center lists a self-service onboarding guide for
> overseas, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan entities, updated August 19, 2026. It
> also lists a review rulebook for overseas and cross-border entities.
> Source: Douyin Enterprise Account Help Center (抖音企业号帮助中心), August 2026. https://renzheng.douyin.com/support/content/227771?graphId=412&mappingType=2&spaceId=77

Both documents sit behind a login, so the papers, fee and review time for that
route aren't public.

A foreign brand name adds one more document.

> A foreign trademark used on an account needs a Chinese registration
> certificate or a Madrid registration certificate.
> Source: Douyin Enterprise Account Help Center, verification information rules, July 2024. https://renzheng.douyin.com/support/content/141500?graphId=412&mappingType=2&spaceId=77

## Video production: the real cost of one shootable week

Douyin (抖音) publishes no posting quota, so cadence is a budget choice. It is the
biggest one you make.

No platform publishes a production price, either. Public procurement does.
Chinese government buyers have to post what they paid, which makes their award
notices the closest thing to a public price list.

| Buyer and date | What they bought | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Beijing Development and Reform Commission, 2025 | At least 50 original short videos, at least 4 a month | 245,000 yuan, about 4,900 a video at most |
| Nanjing university college, 2026 | Promotional film, scripted, shot and graded | 7,500 to 10,000 yuan a finished minute |
| Same contract, 2026 | AI-made promotional video | 3,000 to 8,000 yuan a minute |

> The Beijing commission paid 245,000 yuan for at least 50 original videos, at
> least four a month.
> Source: China National Instruments Import and Export Group (中国仪器进出口集团), award notice, June 2025. https://bid.cnic.com.cn/fjggs/15679.jhtml

> A 2026 award priced promotional film at 7,500 to 10,000 yuan per finished
> minute. AI promotional video ran 3,000 to 8,000 yuan per minute.
> Source: Nanjing University of Finance and Economics Hongshan College (南京财经大学红山学院), result notice, April 2026. https://hs.nufe.edu.cn/2026/0328/c347a26378/page.htm

Now price one shootable week. Say a shoot day gives you four short videos, which
happens to be the Beijing contract's monthly minimum. At Beijing's ceiling, that
comes to about 19,600 yuan. Shoot every week instead of every month and the bill
is four times higher.

So price the video before you price the ads.

There are cheaper routes. [Our pricing page](/pricing/) lists editing and subtitling of
client footage in the entry scope, with real-life shoots in China quoted
separately.

## Live commerce: studio, host and the hidden operating cost

Nobody publishes a dated price for a Douyin (抖音) live studio. The people cost is
public, and it is falling.

> Womenswear hosts with one to two years of experience earned 25,000 to 50,000
> yuan a month in 2023. Now it is about 15,000 to 20,000 yuan.
> Source: Cailian Press (财联社), April 2025. https://www.cls.cn/detail/1992813

> In the fourth quarter of 2025, advertised pay around Beijing averaged 7,300
> yuan a month for short-video operations jobs. E-commerce operations jobs
> advertised 9,700 yuan.
> Source: China Academy of Labour and Social Security Sciences (中国劳动和社会保障科学研究院), February 2026. https://www.calss.net.cn/p1/kybgList/20260224/44935.html

The bigger number is the share of sales a live room eats. Merchants call it the
fee ratio.

> With mid-tier or larger creators, expect slot fees, commission and paid
> traffic to take at least 35% of sales. At big creator sessions, paid traffic
> can be more than 30% of all traffic. A brand's own live room usually runs 10
> points or more lower; one food merchant's runs at 40% to 50%.
> Source: Ebrun (亿邦动力), via Sina Tech, July 2025. https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-07-16/doc-inffsfyq6067212.shtml

| Live commerce line | What the record shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Host | 15,000 to 20,000 yuan a month, womenswear, 1 to 2 years in | Cailian Press, 2025 |
| Operations staff | 7,300 to 9,700 yuan a month, by role | CALSS, Q4 2025 |
| Paid traffic | More than 30% of the traffic at big creator sessions | Ebrun, 2025 |
| Creator session, all in | 35% of sales or more | Ebrun, 2025 |
| Your own live room | 10 points or more below a creator session | Ebrun, 2025 |

The squeeze is getting tighter, too.

## Creator and KOL fees on Douyin

Douyin (抖音) creators are booked through Xingtu (巨量星图), the platform's official
marketplace. It charges its own fee on top.

> Xingtu charges the client a platform fee of 5% on assigned, co-created and
> recruitment tasks, and 10% on open-call tasks.
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), Xingtu settlement rules, June 2026. https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/author/133057

Creators set their own prices against a suggested price, and there is a floor.

> A creator quote below 50% of Xingtu's suggested price may not take effect.
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), Xingtu creator quoting rules, August 2026. https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/author/113055

What do brands actually pay per deal? One outside consultancy puts it in
dollars.

> Average collaborations on Douyin often run 25,000 to 65,000 US dollars,
> against 10,000 to 40,000 US dollars on Xiaohongshu.
> Source: Long Advisory, May 2026. https://longadvisory.eu/en/how-much-influencers-cost-in-china-kol-pricing-explained/

## Douyin Shop commission and fulfillment

If you only want reach, skip ahead to the budget levels. This part is for brands
that want to sell inside the app.

A foreign brand without a mainland company has its own route: Douyin's
cross-border channel, Global Purchase (全球购).

> A cross-border seller needs a company registered outside the mainland, a
> mainland agent that shares liability, and an offshore corporate bank account.
> Mainland-registered companies cannot open this shop type.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), cross-border merchant rules, July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/108056

Douyin calls the commission a technical service fee. It has two parts: a base
rate, plus an extra rate on orders through the Douyin Mall app and Doubao. If an
order is refunded, the fee comes back.

| Category, 2026 | Base rate | Extra channel rate |
|---|---|---|
| Apparel, domestic shop | 5% | 5% |
| Facial care, domestic | 4% | 5.2% |
| Supplements, domestic | 3% | 3.9% |
| Snack food, domestic | 2% | 2.6% |

> In 2026, base and channel rates are 5% and 5% for apparel and 4% and 5.2% for
> facial care. Supplements pay 3% and 3.9%, and snack food 2% and 2.6%.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center (抖音电商学习中心), 2026 rate standards, July 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/aHijyoJA6hvb

Deposits are modest. A shop pays a base deposit of 500, 2,000 or 5,000 yuan, set
by last month's sales. Cross-border sellers add a second deposit on top.

> The base deposit is 500, 2,000 or 5,000 yuan, tiered on the previous month's
> sales.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center, deposit rules, October 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/101830

> Cross-border sellers add an after-sales deposit of twice the base, or ten
> times for high-refund-risk sellers.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center, cross-border deposit rules, June 2025. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/107821

Cash comes back quickly, at least on paper.

> Merchants are paid three days after the buyer confirms receipt, in categories
> with an after-sales period of 30 days or less.
> Source: Douyin E-commerce Learning Center, settlement date rules, September 2026. https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/article/111412

Returns are the cost that bites.

> Douyin e-commerce accounts for 10% to 20% of all e-commerce return parcels in
> China, per 100EC data.
> Source: 21st Century Business Herald (21世纪经济报道), May 2026. https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260519/405e1b01c27de014f97f27670fe0cb1c.html

## Three budget levels and what each realistically achieves

Each level is built from the figures above. We don't add them into one monthly
total, because the creator and live lines grow with your sales. Our own fee for
running them sits on [our pricing page](/pricing/).

| Level | What it includes | Cost anchors | What it can achieve |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Test | Verified account, four videos a month, DOU+ boosts | Up to about 19,600 yuan of production a month at the Beijing rate | Learn what holds attention. No sales engine yet |
| 2. Grow | Weekly shoots, in-feed and search ads, small creators | Four times the test production, plus ads planned from 1,000 yuan a day | Steady reach and search presence. Traffic to your stores |
| 3. Sell | Cross-border shop, your own live room, creator sessions | Hosts and operators every month, plus 35% of sales or more with creators | Sales inside Douyin, if your margin carries the fee ratio |

Level 1 is cheap. It tells you whether Douyin wants your product before you pay
for traffic.

Most brands should sit at level 2 for six months. Ads only work once the videos
do.

The third level is a different business: a shop and a studio, run by a team that
sells every day.

### What a Douyin package should spell out

Whatever the level, a package should name six things in writing:

1. Who verifies the account, and under which entity.
2. How many videos a month, how long, and who shoots them.
3. Ad spend billed at cost, with the platform invoice shown.
4. Which creators, booked where, with the Xingtu fee on the bill.
5. Who staffs the live room, and how many hours a week.
6. Who runs Douyin Shop, and who pays for returns.

[Our Douyin agency page](/douyin-agency/) shows how we cover each one.

Already on Douyin? Hold your monthly bill against these lines. If production is
under four videos a month, the ads have little to work with. If your fee ratio
is past 35% and your gross margin is under 50%, you're below the line one
provider told Ebrun a merchant needs.

## When Douyin is the wrong platform for you

Check the audience first. Douyin (抖音) skews older and more provincial than
Xiaohongshu.

> In October 2025, 22.2% of Douyin users were 51 or older. On Xiaohongshu, 54.2%
> of users were under 30, and 37.2% lived in first-tier and new first-tier
> cities.
> Source: QuestMobile, via 36Kr (36氪), December 2025. https://www.36kr.com/p/3597577937502473

> The Douyin app has more than 500 million monthly users in third-tier cities
> and below.
> Source: QuestMobile, via 36Kr (36氪), July 2026. https://www.36kr.com/p/3884935514648839

Douyin is the wrong first platform if your buyer is young, urban and researching
a considered purchase. Xiaohongshu (小红书) usually fits better. It's also wrong if
your margin cannot carry a live room, or your budget cannot carry weekly video.
One listed Chinese food brand treats it as a launch window.

> Listed Zhongjing Foods treats Douyin and Kuaishou (快手) as launch windows,
> where the goal is to break even with a slight profit.
> Source: 21st Century Business Herald (21世纪经济报道), April 2024. https://www.21jingji.com/article/20240411/herald/2c9861b61345c86a8bdcd2a409a45f4e.html

## FAQ

### What is the minimum budget for Douyin marketing?

The platform minimums are small: 600 yuan for Blue V, 100 yuan for a DOU+ order
and 100 yuan a day for custom search ads. The real minimum is video. One public
contract paid up to about 4,900 yuan a video and required four a month. No first
ad deposit is published, so ask your agency for its rule in writing.

### How much does a Douyin livestream cost to run?

Plan for people and a share of sales. Womenswear hosts with a year or two of
experience earned about 15,000 to 20,000 yuan a month in 2025. Operations jobs
around Beijing advertise 7,300 to 9,700 yuan a month. With creators, slot fees,
commission and paid traffic take 35% of sales or more. Your own room runs about
10 points lower.

### Is Douyin more expensive than Xiaohongshu?

For most brands, yes. Video costs more to make than photo notes (and Douyin
wants it weekly). Long Advisory puts an average Douyin collaboration at 25,000
to 65,000 US dollars, against 10,000 to 40,000 on Xiaohongshu. In return, Douyin
can sell inside the app through live rooms and Douyin Shop.
