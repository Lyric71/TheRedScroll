*Most Chinese car buyers have picked the model before they walk in. Often they've picked the store, too. Automotive marketing in China is won on Douyin (抖音) and Xiaohongshu (小红书), and in the hours after a buyer leaves a phone number.*

China's car market has changed fuel, and its buyers have changed habits. More
than half the new vehicles sold there in 2026 are new energy vehicles, the
Chinese term for electric and plug-in cars. Price cuts no longer set a brand
apart. The work has moved earlier, into the feed, and later, into the lead
follow-up at the dealer.

That holds for a foreign brand like [Jaguar Land Rover](/work/jaguar-land-rover/) and for a Chinese one like
[JAC Motors](/work/jac-motors/). Both are our clients, and you'll meet both below.

Each platform does one job in the purchase.

| Platform                                | Job in the car journey                    | What you publish                   | What you track                 |
|-----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Douyin (抖音)                           | Reach, showroom live streams, local leads | Short video, dealer live streams   | Leads by store, test drives    |
| Xiaohongshu (小红书)                    | Owner proof and lifestyle                 | Owner and creator notes            | Searches for your model, saves |
| Autohome (汽车之家), Dongchedi (懂车帝) | Specs, prices, comparisons                | Model pages, dealer listings       | Comparison traffic, leads      |
| WeChat (微信), WeCom (企业微信)         | The sales chat and owner service          | Service account, rep chats, groups | Contacts added, repeat visits  |
| Weibo (微博)                            | Launch news                               | Reveal posts, hot topics           | Reach in launch week           |

If you haven't launched, read the first three sections. If your accounts are
live and leads are flat, skip to the dealer handoff. Every source on this page
was checked twice in October 2026.

## What changed: the EV shift and a new kind of buyer

The fuel changed first.

> China sold 34.4 million vehicles in 2025, and 16.49 million of them were new
> energy vehicles, up 28.2%.
> Source: China Association of Automobile Manufacturers (中国汽车工业协会), reported by Xinhua (新华社), January 2026. https://www.news.cn/fortune/20260114/cbbd861081c349d8ae238167ca418fa3/c.html

> New energy vehicles reached 47.9% of new vehicle sales in 2025, up 7
> percentage points in a year.
> Source: China Association of Automobile Manufacturers, reported by National Business Daily (每日经济新闻), January 2026. https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-01-15/doc-inhhiusf6561699.shtml

This year the share passed half.

> New energy vehicles took 61.6% of new vehicle sales in September 2026, the
> third month in a row above 60%. From January to September, the share was
> 53.6%.
> Source: China Association of Automobile Manufacturers, reported by Xinhua on People's Daily Online (人民网), October 2026. http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/1012/c1004-40813068.html

> Counting only passenger cars sold to buyers in China, the September share hit
> a record 67.0%.
> Source: China Passenger Car Association (乘联会), reported by China News Service (中新网), October 2026. https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/10-12/10712022.shtml

The home market isn't growing much, though.

> China exported 7.098 million vehicles in 2025, up 21.1%. Without exports,
> domestic sales were 27.302 million, about level with 2017.
> Source: China Economic Net (中国经济网), January 2026. http://auto.ce.cn/auto/gundong/202601/t20260115_2703088.shtml

So more brands chase the same buyers, and those buyers are changing.

> McKinsey's 2026 report on Chinese car consumers found that trading in an
> existing car now drives demand. For the first time, buyers are considering
> fewer brands.
> Source: China Securities Journal (中国证券报), May 2026. https://www.cs.com.cn/ssgs/01/2026/05/13/detail_2026051310011332.html

That's the starting point for EV marketing in China, and a warning for brands
that sell mostly gasoline cars there. Most buyers already own a car. They know
what they like, and their shortlist is getting shorter. If your brand isn't on
it before they start, a showroom discount won't put it there.

Discounts are under pressure anyway.

> China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (工业和信息化部) said
> in May 2025 that price wars have "no winners" and promised tougher action
> against them.
> Source: Xinhua (新华社), May 2025. http://www.news.cn/fortune/20250531/890321bef3b34359a59a84f141c637ff/c.html

Our two automotive clients show both sides of this. Jaguar Land Rover had a
famous name and the wrong age profile.

> "In China, the problem was age."
> Source: TheRedScroll, Jaguar Land Rover case study, October 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/work/jaguar-land-rover/

JAC Motors had the opposite problem: "over 10 million vehicles sold worldwide",
says its case study, and a feed nobody answered.

> "Low comments, low shares, no real sense of community."
> Source: TheRedScroll, JAC Motors case study, October 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/work/jac-motors/

## Where Chinese buyers actually research a car

By the time a buyer reaches the showroom, the choice is mostly made.

> In 2025, 76.4% of car shoppers in China already had a clear brand or model in
> mind when shopping, up 7 percentage points from 2024.
> Source: J.D. Power China, 2025 China Sales Satisfaction Index study, July 2025. https://china.jdpower.com/press-releases/2025-china-sales-satisfaction-index-ssi-study

Douyin (抖音) says most of that choice now happens online.

> About 75% of car buyers in 2025 picked the brand, model, and store through
> short video, live streams, and search before visiting.
> Source: Douyin Life Services (抖音生活服务) white paper, reported by IT Home (IT之家), April 2026. https://www.ithome.com/0/943/223.htm

The decision is quick once it starts.

> In J.D. Power's 2026 study of 9,093 new car owners, the largest group of new
> energy vehicle buyers decided within one to two weeks. The largest group of
> gasoline car buyers decided within a week.
> Source: J.D. Power China (君迪), 2026 China car buyer experience study, June 2026. https://china.jdpower.com/zh-hans/press-releases/2026nianzhongguogouchekehutiyanzhishuyanjiu

A week or two is too short to build a reputation from nothing.

Two car apps sit beside the social feeds. Buyers use them to compare specs,
prices, and dealers.

> Autohome (汽车之家) averaged 76.5 million mobile daily users in June 2026,
> according to QuestMobile.
> Source: Xinhua (新华网), August 2026. http://www.news.cn/tech/20260821/882cec1424c84a2c8a74cf683ac1799e/c.html

> Dongchedi (懂车帝) passed 10 million mobile daily users and says 6 in 10
> serious car shoppers use it.
> Source: Securities Times (证券时报), July 2025. https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/2545107.html

A foreign brand needs complete Chinese model pages on both. Start by checking
them. Prices and specs there should match what its own accounts say, and so
should the dealer list.

## Douyin and Xiaohongshu do different jobs for car brands

On Douyin (抖音), the car moves and the dealer sells.

> Douyin reaches 510 million car-interest users among its 700 million daily
> users, according to Judongche (巨懂车).
> Source: Eastday (东方网), on China Daily (中国日报网), February 2026. https://caijing.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202602/02/WS69807502a310942cc499de73.html

Judongche, Jiemian reports, combines Dongchedi (懂车帝) with Douyin's car
business. It serves carmakers and dealers from the first online contact to the
sale.

On Douyin, live streams sell far more cars than short videos.

> About 35% of Douyin car orders in 2025 came from live streams, 34% from in-app
> search, and 6% from short video. About 670,000 car orders were redeemed
> through Douyin's local services that year.
> Source: Douyin Life Services (抖音生活服务) white paper, reported by IT Home (IT之家), April 2026. https://www.ithome.com/0/943/223.htm

Car advertisers are moving money there.

> Car brands raised their share of Douyin creator spend by 3.6 percentage points
> in 2025.
> Source: Topklout (克劳锐), reported by TopMarketing, June 2026. https://www.itopmarketing.com/info22850

Xiaohongshu (小红书) is where owners say what a car is like to live with.

> Xiaohongshu says more than 200 million of its users are interested in cars. It
> had 350 million monthly active users in July 2025, half of them born after
> 1995.
> Source: China Business Journal (中国经营报), August 2025. https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2025-08-04/doc-infiuumz0399380.shtml

> Users run about 400 million car-related searches a month on Xiaohongshu.
> Source: LatePost (晚点LatePost), April 2025. https://www.163.com/dy/article/JU8EPNCN0531M1CO.html

Jaguar Land Rover's content was made in China, for both feeds.

> "All shot and edited in China for the Chinese market. Not adapted global
> campaigns."
> Source: TheRedScroll, Jaguar Land Rover case study, October 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/work/jaguar-land-rover/

The mistake we see most often is a brand running Xiaohongshu like Douyin. It
posts showroom clips where readers came for an owner's honest review. Side by
side, the split looks like this.

|                      | Douyin (抖音)                            | Xiaohongshu (小红书)                       |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| The buyer's question | Which car, which store, what deal        | What is it like to own                     |
| Who posts            | The brand, dealers, car creators         | Owners, lifestyle creators                 |
| Formats that work    | Showroom live streams, test drive videos | Owner notes, comparisons, road trip photos |
| The next step        | A lead card or deposit with a store      | A search for the model, a save             |
| What to measure      | Leads by store, test drives booked       | Model searches, saves, owner posts         |

## Test drive leads: the handoff from social to the dealer

Douyin (抖音) turns out leads in bulk.

> Douyin had more than 30,200 active car stores in 2025 and produced more than
> 810,000 car sales leads a day, according to Judongche (巨懂车). Stores stream
> and collect buyer details through Dongche Cloud Store (懂车云店), a back-end
> tool.
> Source: Jiemian (界面新闻), September 2026. https://www.jiemian.com/article/15098787.html

Not all of them were real, Douyin says. It changed the rules this autumn.

> From September 1, 2026, brands and dealers can no longer add WeChat (微信) IDs
> to Douyin leads by hand. Buyers who leave a number on a live stream lead card
> must also enter a text message code. The store cannot turn that step off. The
> notice blamed large numbers of fake leads from WeChat IDs entered by stores.
> Source: Jiemian (界面新闻), September 2026. https://www.jiemian.com/article/15098787.html

Expect fewer leads and more real ones. That puts more weight on what happens
next.

And the dealer on the other end is under strain.

> 55.7% of China's car dealers lost money in 2025, and only 23.5% made a profit.
> New car sales contributed -25.5% of dealer gross profit.
> Source: China Automobile Dealers Association (中国汽车流通协会) survey, reported by China Economic Net (中国经济网), March 2026. http://m.ce.cn/qc/gd/202603/t20260325_2856106.shtml

> The same association's inventory warning index stood at 63.2% in September
> 2026, above its 50% boom-bust line. Dealers reported falling foot traffic and
> leads, and longer times to close.
> Source: China Automobile Dealers Association (中国汽车流通协会), September 2026. https://www.cada.cn/Data/info_85_10576.html

Who gets the lead also depends on how the brand sells.

> China had 32,432 4S dealerships at the end of 2025, down 1.4%. Separate new
> energy sales outlets grew about 21% to 26,260. More than half of their sales
> outlets now sell direct, through agency or company-owned stores.
> Source: China Automobile Dealers Association survey, reported by China Economic Net (中国经济网), March 2026. http://m.ce.cn/qc/gd/202603/t20260325_2856106.shtml

A 4S store (4S店) is China's full-service franchised dealership: sales, service,
spare parts, and feedback under one roof. A franchised brand hands the lead to
one. Brands that sell direct keep it in-house, with their own stores or sales
team. Either way, someone has to call back the same day, and the brand has to
see what happened. Agree in the dealer contract who holds the lead data. That's
our working rule. No platform sets one.

Already live, and lead counts fell in September? Check the date before you blame
the content or the agency. The new rule alone removes every lead that existed
only as a WeChat ID typed in by the store. The notice says many of those were
fake. Jiemian adds that the extra text code may lose some buyers halfway. So
compare test drives booked across the change, not raw leads. If test drives held
steady while leads fell, nothing broke.

Douyin's ad platform has a tool for tracking the rest.

> Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), Douyin's ad platform, describes its Feiyu tool as a
> sales-lead tool that assigns and tags leads and connects to other CRM systems.
> A July 2022 update added one-tap adding of a lead in WeCom (企业微信).
> Source: Ocean Engine (巨量引擎), Feiyu CRM App Store listing, confirmed October 2026. https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id1428346102

The whole path, from first video to the keys:

| Stage              | What the buyer does                 | Content that works                | Platform                                                      | The handoff        |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Awareness          | Notices a model                     | Reveal film, creator first drives | Douyin, Weibo (微博)                                          | Follow, search     |
| Research           | Compares specs and owners           | Owner notes, spec comparisons     | Xiaohongshu (小红书), Autohome (汽车之家), Dongchedi (懂车帝) | Save, compare      |
| Shortlist          | Picks a model and a store           | Dealer live stream, local offer   | Douyin                                                        | Lead card, deposit |
| Test drive         | Books and visits                    | Reminder, route, rep intro        | WeCom                                                         | Rep adds the buyer |
| Purchase and after | Signs, takes delivery, gets service | Owner group, service tips         | WeChat, WeCom                                                 | Referral, trade-in |

## Owner communities and the private domain layer

Once the keys change hands, the conversation moves to WeChat (微信) and WeCom
(企业微信).

"Private domain" is the Chinese marketing term for the audience a brand can
reach directly: WeChat followers, WeCom contacts, groups, and app users. The
brand doesn't have to buy reach to talk to them again.

Carmakers are building it in apps.

> Carmakers keep building their own direct channels through official apps and
> WeChat Mini Programs. In June 2026, BYD's official app had 15.835 million
> monthly active users, Great Wall's 4.325 million, and Geely Galaxy's 2.764
> million.
> Source: QuestMobile, reported by DoNews, August 2026. https://www.donews.com/news/detail/1/6666159.html

An imported brand with fewer owners in China can start on WeChat instead. A
service account serves owners, and each buyer gets a rep in WeCom. Its video
channel already carries the carmakers.

> By late 2024, every carmaker had a WeChat Channels (视频号) account, and more
> than 70% had streamed live on it. Dealer accounts had grown 735% in a year.
> Source: China Auto News (中国汽车报), November 2024. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20241108A0796C00

The rep and the owner keep talking in WeCom (企业微信). Sales staff change jobs,
but in WeCom the owner's chat stays with the store.

> A company can move an active employee's customers and customer groups to a
> colleague, and they become contacts automatically after 24 hours.
> Source: WeCom help center (企业微信帮助中心), September 2026. https://open.work.weixin.qq.com/help2/pc/14704

Owners post about their cars anyway, and it's worth asking them to. Jaguar Land
Rover's program did.

> "We set up hashtag challenges and contests to get real owners and fans making
> their own JLR content."
> Source: TheRedScroll, Jaguar Land Rover case study, October 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/work/jaguar-land-rover/

JAC's content put owners on screen as well, with "owner spotlights showing what
JAC vehicles look like in daily life". [Our CRM and Private Domain Traffic
service page](/services/crm-private-domain/) covers the WeCom setup, the groups, and the CRM link behind this.

## Launch campaigns versus always-on content

Launch week is what car brands plan around, from the reveal to the first creator
drives.

Weibo (微博) still earns its place on that week.

> Weibo's ad revenue fell 1% in the second quarter of 2026, but car brands and
> internet services spent more.
> Source: The Paper (澎湃新闻), on 21st Century Business Herald (21财经), August 2026. https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260819/herald/aa73a001934288af9e21e49f5b527807.html

But a buyer who decides in one to two weeks may start looking in any week of the
year. Always-on content is what they find then. JAC's turnaround came from the
slow work.

> "It took a few months. But eventually the shift was obvious. Comments got
> longer."
> Source: TheRedScroll, JAC Motors case study, October 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/work/jac-motors/

Then a campaign landed on a community that was already listening. It wasn't a
model launch. JAC's J-Bot mascot went up in six Olympic designs, and the brand's
own teams abroad picked it up.

> "JAC's international teams saw the J-Bot content and started reposting it on
> Facebook and Instagram on their own."
> Source: TheRedScroll, JAC Motors case study, October 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/work/jac-motors/

|                 | Launch campaign                           | Always-on                                   |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Goal            | Make a new model known fast               | Be there when a buyer starts looking        |
| Length          | The weeks around the reveal               | Every week, all year                        |
| Platforms       | Weibo, Douyin (抖音), creator test drives | Xiaohongshu (小红书), Douyin, WeChat (微信) |
| Content         | Reveal film, first drives, live launch    | Owner stories, how-tos, dealer streams      |
| What to measure | Reach, searches for the model             | Leads, test drives, owner posts             |

Run both. Plan the always-on calendar first (it's the one buyers meet in most
weeks), then drop the launch into it.
