## Locale work, in required order

Propagated to `blog-fr`, `blog-zh`, `blog-de` and `blog-es`, then ran
`deep-translate`, all three passes, FR then ZH then DE then ES. The native
rewrite was not skipped on any locale, and the SEO fields were rewritten in
each pass alongside the body rather than carried over from English.

Two conventions were decided without asking.

1. **FR spacing.** `TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md` section 7 asks for non-breaking
   spaces before `: ; ! ?` and inside guillemets. All 18 published FR articles
   contain zero U+00A0. Consistency across the corpus won, so this article ships
   regular spaces like its neighbors. Flagged here rather than silently
   diverging in either direction.
2. **ZH quotation marks.** The first ZH draft used corner brackets 「」. The
   repo majority is full-width double quotes “ ” by 32 to 10, which is also what
   the guide specifies for zh-CN. Converted.

Terminology fixed per locale and used consistently: FR *honoraires mensuels /
forfait projet / périmètre fixe, prix fixe*, and *avenant* for a change order;
ZH 月费 / 项目制 / 固定范围、固定价格, and 变更单; DE *Monatspauschale /
Projekthonorar / fester Umfang, fester Preis*, and *Nachtrag*; ES *iguala /
tarifa por proyecto / alcance cerrado, precio cerrado*, and *anexo*. ES keeps
the *usted* register the existing ES articles use.

One banned word was caught during drafting rather than after: the FR rendering
of "reaching for external fixes" first came out as *solutions externes*, and
*solutions* is on the STYLE_GUIDE list in every locale. Rewritten to *ceux qui
cherchent la réponse à l'extérieur*.
