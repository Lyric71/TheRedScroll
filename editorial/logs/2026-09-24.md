## Decisions and substitutions

1. **Community hours and reply times are not in the article.** The brief asks
   for them "from our own delivery scopes". The site publishes neither, and
   the ledger has neither. Per SPEC exception 1 they were cut, not estimated.
   A `TODO: unsourced claim removed` comment sits in section 4, and the body
   says plainly that we don't publish those two numbers. The section is built
   on what the site does publish: comment and DM replies, an agreed response
   playbook, same-day escalation, and a crisis plan "within hours". If
   TheRedScroll wants to commit to an hours figure or a reply window in
   public, it should go on a service page first, then into this article.
2. **Meta description rewritten.** The approved meta promised "community
   hours". Rewritten to 150 characters, inside the 152 ceiling: "A
   deliverable-by-deliverable look at an entry-level China social media
   package: posts per platform, community replies, reporting, and what's left
   out." Title kept as approved.
3. **Banned word in the brief's outline.** Section 4 was "Community
   management", and the secondary query "china social media management
   pricing" contains the same root. Both avoided. The H2 is "Community work".
   The secondary query does not appear, same decision as 02A.
4. **Point of contact.** The pricing page says "Dedicated account manager".
   "Manager" carries the banned root and "Lead" is a tier name, so the article
   says "dedicated point of contact".
5. **Who pays platform verification fees.** The site says we open and
   register accounts. It does not say whether the platforms' verification fees
   are passed through or absorbed. The article says only what is sourced: the
   paperwork is part of the package, and the fees are the platforms' own,
   billed per application. Reviewer to confirm this matches the SOW.
6. **Brand mention monitoring.** The Weibo agency page lists "monitor brand
   mentions daily" under community work. The pricing page keeps brand mention
   monitoring out of the entry package. The article treats replies and DMs on
   the client's own accounts as included, and monitoring what is said
   elsewhere as social listening, which is not included. Noted under SPEC
   exception 4 so the two pages can be reconciled.
7. **Larger packages described, never named.** Section 7 gives deliverables
   only ("the next package up", "the largest"). No tier name, no price, and no
   dollar sign anywhere in the file (searched).
8. **Influencer costs.** The ledger's Long Advisory KOL ranges were not used,
   because an English consultancy selling China marketing advice is close to a
   competitor. The Xingtu 5% platform fee was used instead, from the
   platform's own document.
9. **Body length.** 2,276 words with tables against 1,800, about 26% over.
   The brief says "no external stats needed", but SPEC puts every figure in a
   blockquote. So 15 quotes (519 words) and six tables (566 words) sit on top
   of 1,191 words of prose. Nothing was padded. Cutting quotes would cut
   sourced cost lines from a cost page. Same finding as 01D and 02A: briefs
   with this many citable figures need a higher target.
10. **Straight quotes kept** over the quality skill's curly quotes, see step 2.
11. **Model.** Every step ran on Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5), the model this run
    was launched on. No faster mode was offered or used.
