## Blurry hero images fixed (01A, 01B, 01C)

Cyril flagged the three published heroes as blurry on the insights cards and
asked for a fix on each post and a guard so it does not happen again.

**Cause.** Not the site. The cards and article heroes use plain `<img>` with
the 1536x1024 files, enough pixels for a ~350px card. The softness was in the
pixels: 100% crops showed smeared screen text and fuzzy edges. Two reasons:

1. The Sept 4 look rule told every prompt to ask for "slight motion blur, a
   smudged screen, sensor noise" and, for 01B, shallow depth of field. The
   model obeyed.
2. The webp encode at quality 78 flattened what fine detail was left.

**Fix on each post.** Same scenes, same prompts, with the blur, smudge, noise
and shallow-focus phrases replaced by a sharp-focus sentence. gpt-image-2,
quality high, 1536x1024. Each image opened whole and as a 100% crop before
acceptance. Converted with the new `editorial/scripts/convert-hero.mjs`
(webp q90, no resize). The q90 webp crop was checked against the PNG: no
visible loss.
