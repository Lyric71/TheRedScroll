### Searched and rejected

- **Mini Program verification fee amount (300 yuan enterprise, 30 yuan
  individual).** Found only in WeChat Open Community user threads (no
  fetchable official reply), Zhihu, CSDN and vendor blogs. The official
  verification guide confirms a non-refundable review fee and the annual
  review, but its static text gives no amount. The article states the rule
  and gives no Mini Program figure.
- **Tencent Ads minimum first deposit (1,000 yuan direct, 5,000 yuan through
  agents).** Vendor blogs only (ggyzd.com, tengxundsp.com). The Tencent Ads
  help center pages are JavaScript-rendered and return no text to curl or the
  fetch tool. Brief item "Ad account deposit, cite the platform" could not be
  met. Cut in the ads section (the unused 05A draft carried a marker there; never in output/). What the
  platform does publish (free account opening, the bidding and scheduled
  floors) carries the section.
- **Moments Channels CPM ranges and WeChat search ad prices.** The old
  wechat-advertising-formats-costs article cites Zhihu and vendor blogs. Not
  reused. Our advertising page's Channels line ("CPM ¥2 to ¥13") was also not
  used: it is far below every other WeChat figure on the same page and has no
  sample behind it. Closed 2026-10-05 by the publish run (see Items found and
  closed).
- **Custom Mini Program build quotes.** Government procurement notices
  (Jiaxing, Chaozhou) time out from this machine. Every other range found is
  a development shop quoting itself (CSDN, 163 press release, dls6.com,
  ynhl.net). Cut in the Mini Program tier
  table. The table anchors the simple and commerce tiers on Tencent CloudBase
  and Youzan published prices and leaves the custom tier unpriced.
- **Content production cost per article and a copywriter or community salary
  benchmark.** No dated non-vendor source found in this run. Section 3 and the
  WeCom people-cost paragraph carry no market figure; they point to our
  pricing page by name and say plainly why no number is given.
- **Agency retainer market ranges.** Already rejected on 2026-09-03 (brief
  01A). Not researched again. Section 7 uses the approved 0-of-37 claim, the
  Tencent rebate clause and the Advertising Law contract rule.
- A WeCom "charge by number of added contacts from June" story (crm.bytell.cn)
  is an SCRM vendor headline about the 2022 licence change. Not cited.
