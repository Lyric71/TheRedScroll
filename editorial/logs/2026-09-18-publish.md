## Locale work, in required order

FR, then ZH, then DE, then ES. Each locale got the two-step process from
`TRANSLATION_GUIDE.md` section 4 on every section: the humanized translation,
then the mandatory native rewrite done in-language without looking back at the
English. No locale stopped at step 1.

Conventions held to the existing corpus rather than invented:

- Blockquote `Source:` lines stay in English in every locale, publisher names,
  dates and URLs untouched. Only the quoted sentence is localized. This is the
  beauty-skincare precedent and it keeps the citation auditable.
- FR: Le Monde register, guillemets, decimal commas, `%` spaced. Plain space
  before `:` and `?`, following the existing FR corpus rather than diverging
  alone on the non-breaking space; same open item first logged on 2026-09-10.
- ZH: 财经 / 36氪 register, full-width punctuation throughout, zero half-width
  Latin punctuation inside Chinese sentences, numbers and Latin product names
  half-width. Body carries no straight quotation marks.
- DE: FAZ / Handelsblatt register, low-9 and high-99 quotes, decimal commas,
  thousands separated with points.
- ES: El País register, opening `¿` on all three FAQ questions and everywhere
  else a question appears, `%` spaced, decimal commas.
- Brand and product names stay canonical in all five: TheRedScroll, Camper,
  Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat, Weibo, Pugongying.
- The `0 out of 37` competitive line is localized in each locale, matching the
  wording already used on the beauty-skincare pages so the claim reads the same
  across the corpus.

Article ids stay the shared English id across locales, which is what the routing
and hreflang pairing require. The URL segment is localized by the slug map:
`/industries/`, `/fr/secteurs/`, `/zh/hangye/`, `/de/branchen/`,
`/es/sectores/`.
