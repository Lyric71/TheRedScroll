and normalizing whitespace, a script compared it with the draft (with the one

  standfirst also opens the body in italics, as on the other industry pages.

  `platforms` rednote, douyin and wechat. `keywords` holds the brief's primary

  renders the accordion and emits FAQPage schema.

  heading and its paragraph stay, as on food-beverage.

  study (twice), the pricing page (twice), Content Production, Influencer

## Locale work, in the required order

Each locale got a humanized translation first (TRANSLATION_GUIDE step 1).
Then `deep-translate` (the project copy in `.claude/skills/`) ran its three
passes, in the order FR, ZH, DE, ES:

- **Pass 1** re-authored every section, the FAQ and the SEO fields from the
  locale text.
- **Passes 2 and 3** worked only from the locale text, never the English.

The run is unattended, so the skill's 15-second pauses and its
section-by-section display were not used. TRANSLATION_GUIDE §5 iteration 7
("small human imperfections") ran as a cadence pass only, because the house
rule against deliberate errors wins (editorial `CLAUDE.md`). The 22 English
`Source:` lines were inserted by script from the English file, so no citation
was retyped. `Source:` lines stay in English in every locale, as in the
industries corpus.

- **FR** (vous): Le Monde / Les Echos register. It follows the industries-fr
  corpus: straight apostrophes, « », "3 000", "20,8 %" and a plain space
  before ":" and "?".
  - Pass 2 restored the exact Trip.com figure. Pass 1 had written "dépassé de
    40 %", which is equivalent but not the source's wording, so it became
    "franchi la barre des 140 %". Pass 2 also split the Spring Festival
    sentence, which had blurred the two figures, and rewrote the review-rules
    quote and the "qui / quand" bridge.
  - Pass 3 tightened six sentences (the OTA name match, the KOL budget line,
    the revenue-management line). It also aligned the Marriott quotes to the
    FR case page's own wording.
- **ZH** (你): 财经 / 36氪 register. Platform names are in characters only,
  as in the ZH corpus (小红书, 抖音, 携程, 飞猪, 美团, 大众点评). Numbers use
  亿 and 万, with spaces around Latin and digits, and “ ” quotes. The text is
  unwrapped, so line breaks put no stray spaces between characters. A script
  found zero half-width punctuation marks inside Chinese sentences.
  - Pass 1 lifted the step-1 draft to 决策链路, 盘子 and 失策.
  - Pass 2 rewrote 酒店出现了四次，却…, the Marriott framing and the
    跟团游 seller sentence.
