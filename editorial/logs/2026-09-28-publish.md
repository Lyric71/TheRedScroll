## Locale work, in required order

Propagated to `blog-fr`, `blog-zh`, `blog-de` and `blog-es` as a humanized
translation. Then `deep-translate` ran all three passes, in the order FR, ZH,
DE, ES:

- Step 1 fully re-authored each locale in its target register (Le Monde /
  Les Echos, 财经 / 36氪, FAZ / Handelsblatt, El País).
- Steps 2 and 3 were in-language passes that did not look back at the English.

No locale stopped at the humanized translation. The SEO fields were rewritten
in each language. Two parts of the skill were skipped because this run is
unattended: the 15-second pauses and the section-by-section before/after
display.

Fixes made during the passes:

- FR: "solution interne" became "piste interne", because "solutions" is on
  the banned list in every locale.
- ZH: comment moderation is 评论审核, not 评论管理, which avoids the "manage"
  calque.
- DE: "Weitere Artikel erschienen ..." could be read as past tense or as
  subjunctive, so it became "würden ... erscheinen". An ambiguous "sie"
  (agency or list) was resolved.
- ES: "gestión / gestionar" (the "manage" calque) was avoided. Running the
  ads is "ejecución de las campañas", and a sub-client "opera" its account.
  The step 2 pass also removed a "por tanto tanto" repetition.

Conventions carried from earlier publishes:

- FR: typographic apostrophes and regular spaces (the FR corpus has no
  U+00A0).
- ZH: 你, no spaces between CJK and Latin text, platform names in Chinese only,
  full-width “ ” quotes, `来源：` lines.
- DE: Sie, "Prozent" in prose and "5 %" in tables, 1.000 style, Xiaohongshu
  notes as "Beiträge", "Service Account" (the DE corpus majority).
- ES: usted, "5 %" with a space, 1.000 style, and an opening ¿ on every
  question.
