# WeChat marketing cost: account, ads, Mini Program, and WeCom

WeChat marketing cost is mostly yours to set. Tencent's own fees are small: 99
US dollars a year to verify an overseas account. The real money goes to
content, ads, a Mini Program build, and staff.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

Ask what WeChat costs and you'll usually get one number. There isn't one.
WeChat (微信) bills in small, separate pieces, each with its own rules.

For every line, the table below shows what Tencent charges and who sets the
rest. Each figure is sourced further down, and every source was checked twice
in October 2026. Already running an account? Use it as an audit.

| Cost line        | What Tencent charges                               | Who sets the rest   | How often          |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Official Account | 99 US dollars per verification (overseas)          | Nobody              | Every year         |
| Content          | Nothing                                            | Your team or agency | Every month        |
| Moments ads      | From 1,000 yuan a day, or 50,000 per booking       | You set the budget  | Per campaign       |
| Mini Program     | 99 US dollars per verification (overseas)          | Your developer      | Build, then yearly |
| WeChat Pay sales | 0.6% to 1% of each transaction                     | Nobody              | Every sale         |
| WeCom            | 300 yuan to verify (mainland); 2,000 contacts free | Your tool provider  | Every year         |
| Agency fees      | Nothing                                            | The agency          | Every month        |

## What WeChat marketing cost covers: four doors, four bills

First, the reach you're paying for.

> WeChat and its international version had 1.418 billion combined monthly
> active accounts at the end of 2025, up 2% from a year earlier.
> Source: Tencent Holdings 2025 results, reported by Tencent News (腾讯新闻), March 2026. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260318A07APT00

You reach that audience through four products, each billed on its own. You
publish through an Official Account (公众号) and buy ads through Tencent Ads
(腾讯广告). If you sell, you'll want a Mini Program (小程序), the store or app
that lives inside WeChat. And WeCom (企业微信) is where your staff message
customers one to one.

You don't need all four on day one. Most brands open with the account and
content. Ads come once the account has something worth showing. A Mini Program
comes when they're ready to sell.

## Official Account: verification fee, renewal, and the overseas route

Your Official Account (公众号) has one fee that matters: verification. For a
foreign company it isn't optional.

The route depends on where your company is registered. Register outside the
mainland and you file as an overseas entity, paying in dollars.

> An overseas entity pays 99 US dollars per verification application. The fee
> is charged whether or not the review succeeds.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), overseas entity verification FAQ, confirmed September 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/190723aQvMR31907233IF7FV.html

A company registered in mainland China pays in yuan.

> A WeChat Official Account pays 300 yuan per verification application, and
> verified status lasts one year. The fee is not refunded if the review fails.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), platform documentation, confirmed September 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/161219JvMNvi161219raieiY.html

There is a second difference, and it matters more than the fee. An overseas
entity gets one account, and only one type.

> An overseas entity can register one account, and overseas regions support
> the service account type only. The account can be used only after
> verification.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), overseas service account registration, confirmed September 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/180312zQjeYV180312FNNbmA.html

| Your company is registered in | Account type            | Verification fee | Review time          | Renewal    |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------|------------------|----------------------|------------|
| Mainland China                | Subscription or service | 300 yuan         | 1 to 3 working days  | Every year |
| Outside mainland China        | Service only            | 99 US dollars    | 7 to 15 working days | Every year |

> An overseas verification is reviewed within 7 to 15 working days after
> complete documents are submitted.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), overseas entity verification FAQ, confirmed September 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/190723aQvMR31907233IF7FV.html

> A mainland verification is reviewed in 1 to 3 working days.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), registration review times, confirmed September 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/120911VrYVrA1312123qyyqI.html

For most readers of this page, then, the account costs 99 US dollars a year.
Put the renewal date in a shared calendar. Lapsed verification is an avoidable
mess.

## Content production: what drives the cost of an article

Tencent charges nothing for content. You pay for all of it.

No dated public source puts a yuan price on one WeChat article, so this page
won't invent one. What it can show you is what the price depends on.

The first limit is volume. A service account (服务号) can push an article to
its followers four times a month.

> A service account, verified or not, can send the follower push notification
> 4 times per calendar month. The count resets at month end and unused pushes
> do not carry over.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), mass messaging rules, confirmed September 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/120911VrYVrA131025QniAfu.html

That works out to about one pushed article a week. Each one needs copy written
in Chinese, not translated. Add images and layout in the WeChat editor. Then
comes a compliance read before it goes out (a claim that's fine in Europe can
break China's Advertising Law). The price moves mostly with who does each
step. Approval rounds at head office add to it, and so does original
photography.

When you compare quotes, compare the same unit: characters per article, images
per article, rounds of revision, and who signs off on compliance.

Ours is published.

> TheRedScroll's packages include one WeChat article a week, up to 1,500
> Chinese characters with three images. Real-life photo and video shoots in
> China are quoted separately.
> Source: TheRedScroll pricing page, September 2026. https://www.theredscroll.com/pricing/

The prices for that scope are on our pricing page, not here.

## Advertising: Moments, Official Account, and Channels, with minimums

All WeChat ads are bought through one system, Tencent Ads (腾讯广告). Opening
the account is free. The floors sit in the campaigns.

> Tencent advertises free account opening for WeChat ads. Moments ads bought
> by bidding start at a 1,000-yuan daily budget and a 30-yuan CPM. Scheduled
> buys start at 50,000 yuan per placement. Local promotion ads start at 1,000
> yuan a day.
> Source: Tencent Ads (腾讯广告), WeChat advertising product page, confirmed October 2026. https://e.qq.com/topic/marketing/industry/weixin/

Those floors are old and stable. Tencent's 2016 self-serve guide gave the same
three numbers.

> The daily budget for a bidding campaign may not be set below 1,000 yuan,
> the bid must exceed 30 yuan per thousand impressions, and a scheduled buy
> may not be set below 50,000 yuan. Scheduled buys book traffic 5 to 28 days
> ahead.
> Source: Tencent (腾讯), WeChat Moments ads self-serve guide, February 2016. https://wximg.qq.com/wxp/temp/MomentsAdsGuidingBook.pdf

The catch is on the same page. Tencent splits Moments (朋友圈) cities into
core, key, and other. The cheaper bidding route is open only in the "other"
group.

> Moments bidding buys are open only in other cities; core and key cities are
> not supported. Bidding applies to the image-and-text format only.
> Source: Tencent Ads (腾讯广告), WeChat advertising product page, confirmed October 2026. https://e.qq.com/topic/marketing/industry/weixin/

If your buyers live in core cities, Moments means the scheduled route. That
starts at 50,000 yuan a booking.

Official Account ads run inside articles as banners, image-and-text units, and
cards. Tencent's page lists the formats but gives them no separate floor.
They're bought through the same Tencent Ads system.

Channels (视频号), WeChat's short-video feed, is newer. Tencent has not
published a current minimum. The only dated figure is from the launch.

> WeChat Channels feed ads launched on July 18, 2022. Contract buys then had
> a minimum budget of 10,000 yuan, with CPM set at 100, 75, or 50 yuan by city
> tier.
> Source: Niaoge Biji (鸟哥笔记), September 2022. https://www.niaogebiji.com/article-186733-1.html

Get today's floor from Tencent Ads or your agency in writing.

| Placement          | How you buy it            | Published floor                                  | Date of the figure |
|--------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Moments, bidding   | Daily budget, CPM bid     | 1,000 yuan a day, 30-yuan CPM; other cities only | Confirmed 2026     |
| Moments, scheduled | Booked 5 to 28 days ahead | 50,000 yuan per placement                        | Confirmed 2026     |
| Moments, local     | Radius around a store     | 1,000 yuan a day                                 | Confirmed 2026     |
| Official Account   | Through Tencent Ads       | None published                                   | n/a                |
| Channels           | Contract or bidding       | 10,000 yuan at launch; none current              | September 2022     |

For a format-by-format walk-through, see our WeChat advertising guide.

## Mini Program: what you pay Tencent, and what you pay a developer

A Mini Program (小程序) has two kinds of cost: small, published platform fees,
and a build quoted by whoever writes the code.

Verification first. Overseas companies pay the same 99 US dollars as for the
account.

> Every overseas Mini Program verification costs 99 US dollars per
> application, charged whether or not it succeeds. Review takes 7 to 15
> working days after complete documents are submitted.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), overseas Mini Program verification FAQ, confirmed October 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/190712yYfY7v190712u2YjQ3.html

There is a shortcut. A verified service account can register and verify a Mini
Program without paying again, but only for the first period.

> A verified service account can quick-register a Mini Program with no
> 300-yuan fee for mainland entities and no 99-dollar fee for overseas ones.
> The Mini Program's first verification period matches the account's; after
> that, its annual review is applied for separately.
> Source: Tencent customer service (腾讯客服), quick registration FAQ, confirmed October 2026. https://kf.qq.com/faq/170705YVZFZZ170705eyI7Rr.html

Then the build. No dated, independent source publishes build prices by
complexity. The ranges we found all came from developers selling builds. The
table below shows what moves the quote instead, and which fees come on top.

| Tier     | What it does                            | What moves the quote               | Platform fees on top                          |
|----------|-----------------------------------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Simple   | Brand pages, store finder, sign-up form | Number of pages, design work       | Verification                                  |
| Commerce | Catalog, cart, checkout                 | Product count, stock, order links  | Verification, WeChat Pay fee on every sale    |
| Custom   | Membership, points, CRM, phone login    | Systems it connects to, data rules | All of the above, plus per-use component fees |

Our pricing page lists membership Mini Programs as a custom quote, for the
same reason.

Selling adds a fee on every order, paid through WeChat Pay (微信支付).

> WeChat Pay charges no application fee. Merchants pay a service fee on each
> transaction, generally 0.6% to 1% depending on business category. The
> application asks for a business license and a corporate bank account.
> Source: WeChat Pay (微信支付), merchant onboarding guide, confirmed October 2026. https://pay.weixin.qq.com/static/applyment_guide/applyment_detail_store.shtml

That business license is a Chinese one. If you don't have a mainland entity,
read our guide to setting up a Chinese entity for social media first.

Some brands skip the build and open a WeChat Store (微信小店) instead.

> Opening a WeChat Store is free, and new merchants can trial with zero
> deposit in more than 3,000 categories. The platform charges a technical
> service fee on each sale, 1% to 5% in most categories, with no monthly or
> annual fee.
> Source: Tencent Marketing (腾讯营销), WeChat Store FAQ, August 2026. https://e.qq.com/faq/wechat-store/growth/faq-zcjc-008/

One small fee trips up developers. Logging in with a phone number costs money
each time.

> Since August 28, 2023, the phone-number quick verification component has
> been paid: 0.03 yuan per successful call, after 1,000 free trial calls per
> Mini Program.
> Source: WeChat Open Platform (微信开放文档), phone number component documentation, August 2023. https://developers.weixin.qq.com/miniprogram/dev/framework/open-ability/getPhoneNumber.html

## WeCom: licensing, setup, and the people cost

That covers selling. Talking to customers one by one is a separate bill.

WeCom (企业微信) is where one-to-one selling happens. Your staff add customers
as contacts, run groups, and send offers. It has three platform costs.

Verification comes first, and it renews yearly.

> WeCom verification costs 300 yuan per application for a company, not
> refunded if it fails. Companies with 1,001 to 10,000 members add a
> 2,700-yuan member-scale fee, and above 10,000 members 29,700 yuan.
> Verification is reviewed every year at the same fee.
> Source: WeCom help center (企业微信帮助中心), verification fees and annual review, confirmed October 2026. https://open.work.weixin.qq.com/help2/pc/18372

Then the contact cap. This is the one that grows with success.

> Each company gets 2,000 external contacts free. Beyond that it buys a
> capacity tier, valid for one year. An unverified company can add only 100
> customers.
> Source: WeCom help center (企业微信帮助中心), external contact capacity, confirmed October 2026. https://open.work.weixin.qq.com/help2/pc/21326

> When the cap took effect on May 19, 2023, 5,000 contacts cost 500 yuan a
> year and 20,000 contacts cost 2,000 yuan a year.
> Source: Sanyi Shenghuo (三易生活) via Tencent News (腾讯新闻), April 2023. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20230425A07YD600

Those are launch prices. Check the current tiers in your WeCom admin console
before you budget.

Third, the tools. Most brands run WeCom through a CRM tool from a third-party
provider. WeCom charges that provider a license for each staff seat, and the
provider collects it from you.

> WeCom interface licenses are priced per account per year. For 1 to 5
> accounts, a basic account costs 5 yuan and a customer-contact account 50
> yuan; for 1,001 to 10,000 accounts, 1 yuan and 10 yuan. A new app gets a
> 90-day free trial.
> Source: Tencent HiFlow (腾讯云HiFlow), WeCom app pricing, rules in force since May 2022. https://hiflow.tencent.com/docs/applications/qiwei-agent-app-shoufeishuoming/

| WeCom cost line       | Price                                              | Paid to                   |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Verification          | 300 yuan a year (mainland), more above 1,000 staff | Tencent                   |
| Contacts above 2,000  | Tiered, yearly (500 yuan for 5,000 at 2023 prices) | Tencent                   |
| Tool license per seat | 5 to 50 yuan a year at the smallest tier           | Tencent, via the provider |
| The tool itself       | Set by the provider                                | The provider              |
| Staff time            | Your payroll or your agency                        | Your team                 |

Where you register matters here too.

> WeCom accounts registered as overseas companies verify with a registration
> certificate in 1 to 3 working days, but some functions are not supported,
> including external payment collection, conversation archiving, and the
> third-party app marketplace.
> Source: WeCom help center (企业微信帮助中心), overseas company verification, confirmed October 2026. https://open.work.weixin.qq.com/help2/pc/16535

The largest WeCom cost is staff time, and Tencent doesn't bill it. A license
runs a few yuan a seat. A fluent person on shift who knows your products costs
far more. Budget the hours first and the software second. Our WeCom guide
covers the setup itself.

## Agency fees: three models and three questions

Last line, and the murkiest. Agency fees are the line with the least public
information. Fixed prices are rare.

> 0 out of 37 agencies analyzed offer fixed-price packages.
> Source: TheRedScroll competitive analysis, April 2026.

Fees come in three shapes. A monthly retainer pays for a set scope. A
percentage of ad spend pays for running campaigns. And some agencies earn on
the media itself, through rebates from the platform.

Rebates aren't a rumor. Tencent's own contract covers them.

> Tencent alone sets and adjusts its rebate and bonus-inventory policy. The
> non-cash part of an ad account, including bonus credit and rebates, cannot
> be refunded.
> Source: Tencent Ads (腾讯广告), advertiser cooperation agreement, confirmed September 2026. https://e.qq.com/contract.html

Before you sign, ask three questions. Whose name is the ad account in? Who
keeps any rebate? And is ad spend paid to Tencent directly or through the
agency?

The same goes for the Official Account. It should be registered to your
company, not the agency's. Our WeChat agency page puts that in writing.

Our own answer on ad spend is on our pricing page: it's paid directly to the
platform, not through us. We don't quote our fees in articles. The full rate
card is on that page.

## Three worked budgets: presence, growth, and commerce

Put the pieces together for an overseas company and three shapes of budget
appear. The platform figures are the ones sourced above. The agency line
points to a rate card rather than a number, because rates differ.

| Cost line             | Presence                         | Growth                                                | Commerce                                                |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Who it fits           | Not yet launched in China        | Account live, needs reach                             | Ready to sell inside WeChat                             |
| Tencent's fixed share | 99 US dollars                    | 99 US dollars, plus media                             | Verification fees, plus a cut of each sale              |
| Official Account      | 99 US dollars a year             | 99 US dollars a year                                  | 99 US dollars a year                                    |
| Content               | 4 pushed articles a month        | 4 pushed articles a month                             | 4 pushed articles a month, plus product content         |
| Ads                   | None                             | Moments bidding, 30,000 yuan for 30 days at the floor | Set by sales targets                                    |
| Mini Program          | None                             | None                                                  | Build quote, then a yearly verification                 |
| Sales fees            | None                             | None                                                  | 0.6% to 1% via WeChat Pay, or 1% to 5% via WeChat Store |
| WeCom                 | Optional                         | 2,000 contacts free                                   | Verification, contact tiers, tool seats                 |
| Agency                | Content scope, see the rate card | Content and ads scope, see the rate card              | Full scope, see the rate card                           |

The presence budget is mostly content. Tencent's share is 99 US dollars.

Growth is where media comes in. Moments (朋友圈) bidding at its floor, run
every day for a month, is 30,000 yuan. Remember the city rule: if your buyers
are in core cities, the scheduled route starts at 50,000 yuan a booking.

Selling changes the math again: a build up front, then a fee on every sale. On
100,000 yuan of sales, WeChat Pay (微信支付) takes 600 to 1,000 yuan. A WeChat
Store (微信小店) takes 1,000 to 5,000 yuan on the same sales, but you skip the
build. Ten thousand phone-number logins, after the 1,000 free ones, cost 300
yuan.

If you already run an account, check one thing before you add spend: when each
verification renews. The account, the Mini Program, and WeCom can each run on
a different date.

## The costs that surprise people

None of these is large. Each is easy to miss until it bites.

- **Failed verifications are not refunded.** The 99 dollars or 300 yuan is
  gone if your documents are wrong. Get them right the first time.
- **Three products, three renewals.** The account, the Mini Program, and
  WeCom each verify every year, on their own clocks.
- **The cheap ad route skips core cities.** Moments bidding is closed in
  Tencent's core and key city groups.
- **Ad credit is one-way.** Bonus credit and rebates in a Tencent ad account
  can't be refunded. Find out who gets them.
- **Success costs money on WeCom.** Past 2,000 contacts, you buy capacity
  every year.
- **An overseas WeCom does less.** No third-party app marketplace, no
  conversation archive.
- **Logins aren't free.** Phone-number sign-in costs 0.03 yuan a time.
- **Pushes are capped.** A service account alerts followers four times a
  month. Extra articles still appear on the profile, but nobody is notified.

## FAQ

### How much does a WeChat Official Account cost per year?

For an overseas company, 99 US dollars a year in verification fees, charged
per application whether or not it passes. A mainland company pays 300 yuan.
That is Tencent's whole bill for the account. The real yearly cost is content:
about four pushed articles a month, written in Chinese. That part depends on
who writes them.

### What is the minimum WeChat ad budget?

Opening a Tencent Ads account is free. Moments ads bought by bidding start at
a 1,000-yuan daily budget with a 30-yuan CPM floor, but only outside Tencent's
core and key cities. Scheduled Moments buys, open everywhere, start at 50,000
yuan per placement. Tencent publishes no current floor for Channels ads, so
ask before you plan.

### How much does a WeChat Mini Program cost to build?

No independent, dated source publishes build prices, and quotes vary with
scope. A simple brand Mini Program costs far less than one with checkout or
membership. The platform fees are fixed. An overseas company pays 99 US
dollars a year to verify, 0.6% to 1% on each WeChat Pay sale, and 0.03 yuan
per phone-number login.
