# Bilibili marketing for foreign brands: audience, cost, and fit

Bilibili marketing suits brands with something worth explaining and the means to
prove it on camera. Budget for production and editing, creator rights, and paid
distribution separately.

<!-- INTRODUCTION -->

Reviewed October 20, 2026.

A launch film can introduce the product. For a creator commission, give viewers
a useful demonstration and explain what the product cannot do.

| Your situation | Starting decision | What to check |
|----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| A product needs a careful demonstration | Consider a creator test | Similar subjects in the creator’s recent work |
| Existing videos get views but few inquiries | Audit before buying more reach | Questions, viewing behavior, and the next step |
| You need immediate sales from an untested channel | Keep the first test narrow | A traceable action and working purchase route |
| You cannot fund original local content | Postpone a dedicated program | Production capacity and Chinese-language replies |

Our October 2026 source review did not establish a universal price for this
work. Ask for a scoped quote, with creator work separated from media spend. The
sections below explain what belongs in that quote.

## Who Bilibili reaches in 2026

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) is a video community that reaches many young adults. Treating it
as a channel only for teenagers can lead to the wrong creative brief.

> Bilibili reported 371 million monthly active users and 113 minutes of
> average daily use in the second quarter of 2026.
> Source: Bilibili Q2 2026 results, August 2026. https://ir.bilibili.com/media/i5yhufw4/hkex-eps_20260827_12302715_0-1.pdf

> The average age of Bilibili’s active users was 26.5 in the second quarter
> of 2026.
> Source: Bilibili Q2 2026 investor presentation, August 2026, page 7. https://ir.bilibili.com/media/xx5dzckf/q2-2026-bilibili-inc-investor-presentation.pdf

Daily use totals time across the service. It does not measure a single viewing
session or predict completion of your film. The average age cannot identify the
buyers following a particular creator.

Review recent ordinary uploads alongside sponsored ones. Compare recurring
topics and product questions; do not judge fit from a single viral hit.

Before entering, examine that creator’s recent subjects and audience data. An
existing account gives you another check. Compare the people asking product
questions with the customers you intended to reach.

They may not match.

## Earn the running time

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) makes room for detailed videos, but length alone is no
advantage. Its annual report also describes short-form Story Mode. Choose the
format around the question, rather than treating the platform as long-form only.

> Bilibili describes Story Mode as its short-form video feature.
> Source: Bilibili 2025 annual report on Form 20-F, April 2026. https://ir.bilibili.com/media/uclpt3pv/2025-annual-report-on-form-20-f-1.pdf

A 20-minute product investigation is an editorial option, not a performance
benchmark. Give it a clear question, then show enough of the test for viewers to
judge the results. A shorter demonstration may do the job.

In a proposed test, a microphone brand could show the same recording task in
different rooms. Let viewers hear the result before explaining the design. An
animated logo sequence adds running time without helping them choose.

If you already publish, review where viewers leave. Check whether the drop
coincides with a repetitive introduction, an unexplained technical term, or the
sponsored passage. Use that finding to revise the next script.

## Give viewers something they can use

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) reaches beyond entertainment. Its learning activity gives brands
a reason to test useful explanations, without proving that every viewer is a
buyer.

> Bilibili reported that 230 million users learned professional knowledge
> on the platform in 2025.
> Source: Bilibili 2025 environmental, social, and governance report, April 2026. https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0416/2026041601383_c.pdf

A consumer electronics brand can compare real use cases. A food brand can teach
a technique and explain why an ingredient behaves differently. An industrial
supplier might demonstrate a maintenance problem. These are proposed tests, not
claims about past campaign results.

Begin with questions a customer would ask before buying. Find a creator who has
already answered related questions well. Check whether the video answers that
question, rather than merely repeating product claims.

The Content Production service page explains the production work. Bring the
equipment and product access requirements into the brief. Assign someone
qualified to check the explanation before filming begins.

<!-- SECTION: Creator relationship -->

## Brief an UP creator without writing their whole script

On Bilibili (哔哩哔哩), an UP creator (UP主) is a content creator. That person can
also be a key opinion leader, or influencer. The terms overlap.

Be clear about the work you commission. A demonstration needs time to use the
product and decide what can honestly be said. A scripted endorsement answers a
different brief.

> Bilibili lists Huahuo (花火) creator commercial orders among its creator
> income channels.
> Source: Bilibili 2025 environmental, social, and governance report, April 2026, page 20. https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0416/2026041601383_c.pdf

Use the platform’s official route to check the proposed deal, alongside the
creator’s recent sponsored work.

Agree on the factual claims and evidence before filming, while leaving room for
the creator’s own explanation. Put usage rights in writing. Publication on their
account does not tell you whether the quote includes paid reuse. Specify where
that reuse is allowed and when permission expires. Include permission for any
shorter edits planned for advertising.

Name the script approver and set a revision limit. Decide what happens if
testing reveals a limitation. Agree on product return terms and the expected
publication window. Allow enough time for the creator to test the product before
the script needs approval.

<!-- SECTION: Advertising -->

## Ads: formats to compare before asking for a price

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) sells several kinds of advertising. A creator fee and an
advertising budget buy different work, even when they support the same video.

> Bilibili’s annual report describes brand, performance, and creator-made
> native advertising. It says prices are set case by case.
> Source: Bilibili 2025 annual report on Form 20-F, April 2026, page 85. https://ir.bilibili.com/media/uclpt3pv/2025-annual-report-on-form-20-f-1.pdf

Brand placements include the app opening page. Performance ads can appear
alongside regular feeds or beneath a video. Native advertising puts a paid
message inside creator content. Confirm the currently available placement and
destination for your category before approving a plan.

| Route | What the quote should cover | The missing detail to catch |
|-------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Creator integration | Production and publication on an agreed account | Reuse rights and revision limits |
| Feed or video-page advertising | Placement, targeting, and media spend | Charge per view, click, or other agreed unit |
| App-opening brand placement | Inventory and campaign dates | Reservation terms and reporting |
| Paid promotion of creator content | Distribution plus permission to use the video | Whether rights cost extra |

Our source check did not establish a current universal opening deposit, daily
minimum, or typical cost per click. A reseller’s starting figure should not
become your assumed platform rule.

The first quote may bundle these charges. Ask the seller to separate a deposit
from actual media spend. Request the applicable minimum in writing and confirm
whether it applies to the account, campaign, or placement. Check which charges
can be refunded, and confirm currency and tax treatment before comparing totals.

## Read the bullet comments in context

When reviewing a Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) video, read its bullet comments (弹幕) alongside
the scene that prompted them.

> Bullet comments are tied to the video’s context and appear across its screen.
> Source: Bilibili 2025 annual report on Form 20-F, April 2026, page 1. https://ir.bilibili.com/media/uclpt3pv/2025-annual-report-on-form-20-f-1.pdf

A comment beside a product claim may express doubt. Several jokes at the same
moment might mean a reference landed, or that the audience is mocking it. A
Chinese-speaking reviewer should watch the scene before assigning sentiment.

Do not paste a popular phrase into a brand script without checking its meaning
in that community. Avoid manufactured praise. Give the creator room to answer
fair criticism, and supply evidence when a question concerns your product.

For reporting, record recurring technical questions and their place in the
video. Separate product interest from jokes about the presenter. Count product
questions separately before treating the conversation as evidence of buying
interest.

| Strong candidate | Food skills, creative software | The audience learns something useful |

questions under the video. Fixing those gaps may help more than changing
creators.

## Build the budget around the work

A Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) program needs a production scope before a useful price
comparison. The choices below are suggested briefs, not market averages for
length or cost.

| Proposed format | Editorial length choice | Main cost driver |
|-----------------|-------------------------|------------------|
| Focused demonstration | As short as the task allows | Product access and repeatable testing |
| Detailed investigation | Longer only where evidence earns the time | Research, specialist review, and editing |
| Creator integration | Fit the creator’s existing format | Audience fit, production, and rights |
| Follow-up explanation | Answer a specific unresolved question | New evidence and additional filming |

Separate research and scripting from filming. Then list editing, Chinese
captions, product shipping, creator fees, rights, and paid media. Give comment
replies and reporting their own scope. Otherwise a cheap-looking quote can leave
important work unpriced. Compare totals only after both proposals include the
same work and rights.

For a first test, keep the audience and product question clear. Decide what
would justify another commission. It might be qualified inquiries or visits to a
working product page, depending on the purchase journey. Define a qualified
inquiry before launch, such as a request from the intended customer about a
relevant product. Test the destination on a phone. Confirm that a viewer can
find the relevant product and complete the intended action.

Existing programs need a different check: compare spend against useful actions,
then inspect what drove the difference between videos. Separate paid
distribution from unpaid viewing in the report, including each source’s views
and spend. Do not credit the creator with sales that cannot be traced to the
campaign.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Bilibili worth it for foreign brands?

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) is worth testing when your product benefits from a useful
explanation and a relevant creator can deliver it credibly. Confirm that viewers
have a practical next step, such as a product page or inquiry route. If you only
have translated promotional footage, develop the local content brief before
committing to a continuing program.

### How much do Bilibili UP hosts charge?

Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) UP creators (UP主) need a scoped quote. This review found no
reliable current public rate range that applies across creators. Ask for
production, publication, revisions, and reuse rights to be stated separately.
Compare proposals for the same deliverable, and keep paid distribution distinct
from the creator’s fee.

### What content works on Bilibili?

Useful demonstrations, explanations, and stories are sensible tests on Bilibili
(哔哩哔哩). Choose a subject the creator’s audience already cares about, then show
something viewers can judge for themselves. Length should follow the question. A
detailed test may need time, while a single practical tip may work better as a
short video. Length alone earns no reach.
